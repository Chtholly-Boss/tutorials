Choosing registers and bits选择寄存器与控制位

The planner turns a site set into probe islands. For every site it decides which dead registers the probe may use, which scoreboards it may claim, in what order its instructions issue and what control bits each one carries. Three planners share the same templates and differ only in how hard they try to be cheap; all three refuse a site they cannot plan within the rules, before anything is launched.

规划器把一组站点变成探针 island。对每个站点，它决定探针可以用哪些死寄存器、可以占用哪些 scoreboard、指令按什么顺序发射、每条指令带什么控制位。三个规划器共用同一套模板，区别只在于为了省周期肯花多少力气；对于不能在规则之内规划的站点，三者都在任何启动之前拒绝。

§1Contract契约

What this layer promises这一层的承诺

A plan whose every probe uses only dead registers and waits only on its own scoreboards; a site that cannot be planned that way is refused before anything is launched.

一份计划，其中每个探针只使用死寄存器、只等待自己的 scoreboard；不能这样规划的站点，在任何启动之前被拒绝。

olympus/planner → hands a交付 rewriter to给 olympus/runtime · takes templates from模板来自 olympus/probes, dead registers from，死寄存器来自 olympus/liveness, distances from，间距来自 olympus/hazards

Every planner is called the same way, plan_case(case, set, …) with the ring depth and the granularity as keywords, and returns a plan with the kernel, the cubin's sha256, the record layout, the sites and a rewriter(image, source); the runtime (chapter 02) calls that rewriter to get the SpliceResult it loads __init__.py:1–18. olympus trace, the benches and the verification tools name the planner by a spec, conservative | exact | search or any module:callable resolve.py:18–24. Without the first half of the promise a probe would change the kernel's outputs, and the runtime's gate would catch it. Without the second half a probe would change the kernel's timing, which is the thing being measured, and no gate catches that.

每个规划器的调用方式都一样，plan_case(case, set, …)，环深度和粒度作为关键字参数，返回的计划带着 kernel、cubin 的 sha256、记录布局、站点，以及一个 rewriter(image, source)；运行时（第 02 章）调用这个重写器，得到它要加载的 SpliceResult __init__.py:1–18。olympus trace、各个 bench 和验证工具用一个 spec 指定规划器：conservative | exact | search，或任何 module:callable resolve.py:18–24。没有承诺的前一半，探针会改变 kernel 的输出，运行时的门会抓住它；没有后一半，探针会改变 kernel 的时序，而时序正是被测量的东西，没有任何门能抓住它。

§2Invariant不变量

Invariant不变量3

A probe never waits on a scoreboard only the kernel claims. Such a wait would turn the kernel's overlapped latency into a stall and change what is being measured. A site with no free scoreboard is refused, never reclaimed.

探针从不等待只有 kernel 占用的 scoreboard。这样的等待会把 kernel 本可重叠的延迟变成停顿，改变被测量的东西。没有空闲 scoreboard 的站点直接拒绝，绝不回收。

Checked by a static check over every wait bit of every probe instruction: each must be justified by a claim the probe made earlier in its island, or by a register it touches that the kernel has pending exact.py:1108–1112; by the rewriter's hazard check of the real spliced cubin, which fails closed; and by the scoreboard scan of the final run, which finds no island waiting on a kernel scoreboard (EVAL.md). The refusal is invariant 5's: a site with no free scoreboard raises ScheduleError before any launch exact.py:90–91; 10 reviewed corpus sites and about 1% of fuzz-generated sites are refused this way (EVAL.md).

检查方式：一个静态检查遍历每条探针指令的每个等待位，每一位都必须有依据：要么是探针在同一 island 中更早登记的占用，要么是它触及的、kernel 仍挂在该 scoreboard 上的寄存器 exact.py:1108–1112；重写器对真实拼接后 cubin 的冒险检查，失败即停；以及最终运行的 scoreboard 扫描，没有发现任何 island 等待 kernel 的 scoreboard（EVAL.md）。拒绝属于不变量 5：没有空闲 scoreboard 的站点在任何启动之前抛出 ScheduleError exact.py:90–91；语料库中 10 个审阅过的站点和约 1% 的模糊测试站点因此被拒绝（EVAL.md）。

The weaker rule is reclamation: when no scoreboard is free, wait early on one the kernel holds so the probe can reuse it. It costs coverage nothing and looks harmless, because the kernel would have waited on that scoreboard eventually. It is not harmless. At DeepGEMM k512's c.tile_done the kernel's TMA stores hold SB0 and the kernel waits on it only one tile later; a single spliced NOP waiting on SB0 there costs +18.4% end to end, about 0.8–0.9 µs per tile, and both the oracle tool and Olympus's own fenced planner had been paying it as a "barrier wait" in their traces (DECISIONS 61). So the rule is absolute, and the price is paid in coverage: the ten corpus sites where the kernel holds all six scoreboards are not traced (DECISIONS 63).

更弱的规则是回收：没有空闲 scoreboard 时，提前等待一个 kernel 占着的 scoreboard，好让探针复用它。它不损失覆盖，看起来也无害，因为 kernel 迟早也要等那个 scoreboard。但它并不无害。在 DeepGEMM k512 的 c.tile_done，kernel 的 TMA store 占着 SB0，而 kernel 自己要到下一个 tile 才等它；在那里只拼接一条等待 SB0 的 NOP，端到端就慢 +18.4%，每个 tile 约 0.8–0.9 µs，而 oracle 工具和 Olympus 自己带围栏的规划器都曾在追踪里把这笔开销当成"barrier 等待"记下来（DECISIONS 61）。所以这条规则是绝对的，代价用覆盖来付：语料库中 kernel 占满全部六个 scoreboard 的十个站点不被追踪（DECISIONS 63）。

§3How it works如何工作

Three planners over the same templates同一套模板上的三个规划器

The conservative planner is the baseline. It places INIT before instruction 1, one RECORD before each site and an EXIT calibration before every exit, picks three persistent registers no kernel instruction touches and per-probe temporaries from the dead set, and fences every probe: a lead NOP waiting on SB0–5, 15-cycle stalls throughout, private SB0 / SB1, a trailing NOP waiting on SB0–5 again conservative.py:40–47. Its correctness depends on no latency model, which is why it exists: it is bit-exact on the whole corpus and is what olympus trace runs by default; it also perturbs timing, which is why the other two exist.

conservative 规划器是基线。它把 INIT 放在第 1 条指令之前，每个站点之前一个 RECORD，每个出口之前一个 EXIT 校准；挑三个 kernel 任何指令都不碰的持久寄存器，再从死寄存器集合里为每个探针取临时寄存器；并给每个探针加围栏：开头一条等待 SB0–5 的 NOP，全程 15 周期的 stall，私用 SB0 / SB1，结尾再一条等待 SB0–5 的 NOP conservative.py:40–47。它的正确性不依赖任何延迟模型，这正是它存在的理由：在整个语料库上逐位一致，也是 olympus trace 的默认；它同时会扰动时序，这又是另外两个存在的理由。

The exact planner takes the same plan and reschedules it hazard-exactly. Every probe instruction gets the smallest stall the hazard model (chapter 06) allows for its pairs: the latency tables and MIN_WAIT_NEEDED, or the measured minimum safe gap where the pair was measured; variable-latency results go through scoreboards drawn from the free set, every claim released before the island ends, and a wait at least SB_SET_GAP cycles after its setter exact.py:18–37. The body is list-scheduled, critical path first; schedule_island(body, ic, order) is the interface the search calls with an order of its own exact.py:806–810.

exact 规划器接过同一份计划，按冒险精确地重新调度。每条探针指令拿到冒险模型（第 06 章）允许的最小 stall：延迟表和 MIN_WAIT_NEEDED，或者在测量过的指令对上用实测的最小安全间隔；可变延迟的结果走 scoreboard，只从空闲集合里取，每个占用在 island 结束前释放，等待至少在设置者之后 SB_SET_GAP 个周期 exact.py:18–37。探针体按关键路径优先做列表调度；schedule_island(body, ic, order) 是搜索规划器带着自己的顺序来调用的接口 exact.py:806–810。

The search planner is Algorithm 1. Per site island it enumerates three decisions jointly: D2, the topological orders of the probe's dependencies; D1, how the probe's values share registers (the proper colourings of their interference graph, placed on the coldest dead registers); D3, which free scoreboard each claim takes. Stalls are not searched; they are derived from the same model the exact planner uses search.py:10–28. Its greedy variant prices each instruction by its critical path and issues the highest price first, in one pass search.py:55–57. It is the default of sites trace and the benches, and the planner every evidence sweep used.

search 规划器就是 Algorithm 1。对每个站点 island，它联合枚举三项决策：D2，探针依赖关系的拓扑顺序；D1，探针的值如何共用寄存器（干涉图的合法着色，放到最冷的死寄存器上）；D3，每个占用取哪个空闲 scoreboard。stall 不参与搜索，而是由与 exact 规划器相同的模型推导 search.py:10–28。它的贪心变体按关键路径给每条指令定价，一遍扫过、价高者先发 search.py:55–57。它是 sites trace 和各个 bench 的默认，也是每一次证据扫描所用的规划器。

The cost is the kernel's own delay开销就是 kernel 自己的延迟

A candidate's cost is o′ = t′[i(p)] − t0[i(p)]: the delay the island adds before the kernel's instruction at the insertion point. That is the provable island length, with every scoreboard wait priced at a measured release latency (the p50s of the measured gaps). A wait on a kernel scoreboard, allowed only for a register the probe touches that the kernel has pending, costs until that operation releases search.py:29–33. The search is depth-first with branch and bound on an admissible bound; its incumbent starts at the better of the exact planner's schedule and the greedy one, so the optimum is never worse than either search.py:35–39.

一个候选的开销是 o′ = t′[i(p)] − t0[i(p)]：island 给插入点处 kernel 指令增加的延迟。也就是可证明的 island 长度，加上每个 scoreboard 等待按实测释放延迟（实测间隔的 p50）计的价。对 kernel scoreboard 的等待，只在探针触及的寄存器仍挂在 kernel 那里时才允许，其代价一直算到那个操作释放为止 search.py:29–33。搜索是带分支定界的深度优先，下界可容许；当前最优从 exact 规划器的调度和贪心调度中较好的一个起步，所以最优解不会比两者中任何一个差 search.py:35–39。

A deterministic work budget确定性的工作量预算

The search is anytime, but it never reads the clock. Each site island gets WORK_BUDGET = 5,000 work units; every DFS node, leaf, dependency edge, kernel-context gap, renamed word and register placement is charged a calibrated number of units, about one microsecond each, so the plan is the same on every machine and in every run search.py:80–96. A site that runs out keeps its incumbent, always a valid schedule, and is reported proven: False with the phase it was capped in. Over the 2,028 sites of the final sweep the search took median 2.99 ms, p95 4.88 ms and at most 6.60 ms per site, none over the 10 ms target; 622 sites were capped, and 615 of those had already reached the optimum (DECISIONS 73).

搜索是 anytime 的，但从不读时钟。每个站点 island 有 WORK_BUDGET = 5,000 个工作量单位；每个 DFS 节点、叶子、依赖边、kernel 上下文间距查询、重命名的指令字和寄存器放置都按校准过的单位数计费，每单位约一微秒，于是计划在每台机器、每次运行上都相同 search.py:80–96。用完预算的站点保留当前最优（总是一个合法调度），报告为 proven: False 并注明在哪个阶段被截断。在最终扫描的 2,028 个站点上，每站点搜索中位数 2.99 ms、p95 4.88 ms、最长 6.60 ms，没有一个超过 10 ms 的目标；622 个站点被截断，其中 615 个其实已经到达最优（DECISIONS 73）。

Fallbacks and refusal回退与拒绝

When the dead registers at the sites do not hold the probe, two fallbacks stand in front of the search. The default downgrades a thread-granularity plan to per-warp: the persistent state moves to three uniform registers, which survive USETMAXREG, an elected lane writes the records, and the temporaries come from the far larger dead UR set. The opt-in raises EIATTR_REGCOUNT by 8, 16 or 32 and gives the probe only the new registers, which the kernel never names; it is refused for kernels with USETMAXREG and may cost occupancy, which is why it is opt-in fallback.py:1–24. Refusal comes in two forms, both before launch: a site whose value cannot be read before the site is left out of the plan with its reason in the report (SiteRefused) conservative.py:103–107, and an island with no free scoreboard is refused by the scheduler (ScheduleError). Both are coverage gaps, never correctness risks.

当站点处的死寄存器放不下探针时，搜索前面有两道回退。默认的一道把线程粒度的计划降级为按 warp：持久状态搬到三个 uniform 寄存器（它们不受 USETMAXREG 影响），由选出的 lane 写记录，临时寄存器取自大得多的死 UR 集合。可选的一道把 EIATTR_REGCOUNT 提高 8、16 或 32，只把新增的寄存器（kernel 从未命名过）给探针；对带 USETMAXREG 的 kernel 它被拒绝，而且可能损失占用率，所以是可选项 fallback.py:1–24。拒绝有两种形式，都在启动之前：值在站点之前读不到的站点被排除在计划之外，原因写进报告（SiteRefused）conservative.py:103–107；没有空闲 scoreboard 的 island 由调度器拒绝（ScheduleError）。两者都是覆盖缺口，不是正确性风险。

What the static sweep proves静态扫描证明了什么

Before a plan is trusted it is checked as a whole, on the CPU. The spliced program, the original instructions plus the islands on the original control flow, goes through the hazard model's check; a violation that touches a probe is repaired, a longer stall or one more wait, and the check repeated until clean exact.py:908–911. The rewriter then checks the real spliced cubin again, decoded with its own control flow, and fails closed. The static sweep runs this for every case × set of the corpus and adds, per planner, the text check of every island word against cuobjdump, sass_depcheck on the spliced kernel against the original, binary similarity (A2), REGCOUNT unchanged (A3), and the per-site report of cost, proof or cap, registers and search time (A8) sweep.py:1–28.

一份计划在被信任之前，要在 CPU 上作为整体检查。拼接后的程序，即原指令加上原控制流上的各个 island，过一遍冒险模型的 check；涉及探针的违例会被修复（更长的 stall，或多一个等待），然后重复检查直到干净 exact.py:908–911。随后重写器用真实拼接后的 cubin 再查一遍，按它自己解码出的控制流，失败即停。静态扫描对语料库的每个用例 × 集合都这样做，并按规划器加上：每个 island 指令字与 cuobjdump 的文本比对、拼接后 kernel 对照原 kernel 的 sass_depcheck、二进制相似度（A2）、REGCOUNT 不变（A3），以及每站点的开销、证明或截断、寄存器与搜索时间报告（A8）sweep.py:1–28。

§4Where it came from从何而来

The paper turns splicing into a search paper §4.2: the overhead of a splicing is the delay it adds to the kernel's instruction at the insertion address, each issue time is the latest predecessor's plus the spacing between them, dynamic dependencies are priced at measured latencies, and three decisions are enumerated jointly, the instruction order, the register assignment and the control bits, keeping the cheapest; a greedy variant prices each instruction by its critical path (Eq. 4) and issues the highest first. Olympus's D1, D2, D3, its cost and its greedy are those. The two register-shortage remedies, downgrading thread-level tracing to per-warp and raising the register count, are the paper's fallbacks paper §4.3. The paper also reports the search's cost in time per site paper §5.6; Olympus set itself the same kind of target, at most 10 ms per site, and met it.

论文把拼接变成一个搜索问题 论文 §4.2：一种拼接的开销是它给插入地址处 kernel 指令增加的延迟；每条指令的发射时间取最晚前驱加上二者间距；动态依赖按实测延迟计价；三项决策联合枚举，即指令顺序、寄存器分配和控制位，保留最便宜的；贪心变体按关键路径给每条指令定价（式 4），价高者先发。Olympus 的 D1、D2、D3、开销定义和贪心都来自这里。两种寄存器不足时的补救，把线程级追踪降为按 warp，以及提高寄存器数，是论文的回退 论文 §4.3。论文还以每站点时间报告搜索的代价 论文 §5.6；Olympus 给自己定了同类目标，每站点最多 10 ms，并达到了它。

Where Olympus departs: a work budget, not a clockOlympus 的不同之处：工作量预算，而不是时钟DECISIONS 73

A search bounded by wall-clock time gives a different plan on a different machine, or on the same machine on a busy day, and the spliced image changes with it. Olympus bounds the search by counted work: 5,000 units per site, each operation charged a calibrated cost, so the plan is a pure function of the cubin and the sites. The calibration keeps a unit near one microsecond, which is how the budget stays under 10 ms per site while never reading the clock DECISIONS.md:364–369.

以墙钟时间为界的搜索，换一台机器，或者同一台机器赶上忙的一天，就会给出不同的计划，拼接后的镜像也随之改变。Olympus 用计数的工作量为界：每站点 5,000 个单位，每种操作按校准过的代价计费，于是计划是 cubin 和站点的纯函数。校准让一个单位接近一微秒，这就是预算既能把每站点控制在 10 ms 以内、又从不读时钟的原因 DECISIONS.md:364–369。

Where Olympus departs: no reclamationOlympus 的不同之处：不回收DECISIONS 63

The paper's reference tool reaches a site with no free scoreboard by waiting on one the kernel holds; that is how it traces the ten corpus sites Olympus does not (EVAL.md). Olympus draws scoreboards only from the free set, waits on a kernel scoreboard only for a register the probe itself touches, and refuses the site otherwise; the first hazard-exact scheduler had reclaimed in 117 islands and reproduced exactly the artifact of DECISIONS 61, so reclamation was dropped DECISIONS.md:227–232.

论文的参考工具靠等待 kernel 占着的 scoreboard 来到达没有空闲 scoreboard 的站点；语料库里 Olympus 不追踪而它追踪的十个站点就是这么来的（EVAL.md）。Olympus 只从空闲集合取 scoreboard，只在探针自己触及的寄存器挂在 kernel scoreboard 上时才等它，否则拒绝该站点；第一版按冒险精确调度的调度器曾在 117 个 island 里做过回收，并恰好复现了 DECISIONS 61 的伪影，于是回收被放弃 DECISIONS.md:227–232。

Beyond the paper: three planners and a sweep论文之外：三个规划器和一次扫描

The fenced planner, the hazard-exact list scheduler and the static sweep are Olympus's. The paper describes the fenced approach only as the naive splicer it improves on; Olympus built it first, on purpose, as the correctness baseline the other two are measured against, and kept it as the olympus trace default. On the final pass the greedy order equals the optimal one on 1,603 of 1,644 T1–T4 sites; all 31 worse sites use a borrowed or saved predicate (DECISIONS 75).

带围栏的规划器、按冒险精确的列表调度器和静态扫描是 Olympus 自己的。论文只把围栏方法当作它所改进的朴素拼接器来描述；Olympus 有意先把它做出来，作为另外两个规划器据以衡量的正确性基线，并保留为 olympus trace 的默认。在最终一轮上，贪心顺序在 1,644 个 T1–T4 站点中的 1,603 个上与最优相同；差的 31 个站点都用了借来的或保存过的谓词（DECISIONS 75）。
