Choosing registers and bits选择寄存器与控制位

The planner turns a site set into probe islands. For every site it decides which dead registers the probe may use, which scoreboards it may claim, in what order its instructions issue and what control bits each one carries. Three planners share the same templates and differ only in how hard they try to be cheap; all three refuse a site they cannot plan within the rules, before anything is launched.

规划器把一组站点变成探针 island。对每个站点，它决定探针可以用哪些死寄存器、可以占用哪些 scoreboard、指令按什么顺序发射、每条指令带什么控制位。三个规划器共用同一套模板，区别只在于为了省周期肯花多少力气；对于不能在规则之内规划的站点，三者都在任何启动之前拒绝。

§1Contract契约

What this layer promises这一层的承诺

A plan whose every probe uses only dead registers and waits only on its own scoreboards; a site that cannot be planned that way is refused before anything is launched.

一份计划，其中每个探针只使用死寄存器、只等待自己的 scoreboard；不能这样规划的站点，在任何启动之前被拒绝。

olympus/planner → hands a交付 rewriter to给 olympus/runtime · takes templates from模板来自 olympus/probes, dead registers from，死寄存器来自 olympus/liveness, distances from，间距来自 olympus/hazards

Every planner is called the same way: plan_case(case, set, …), with the trace buffer's ring depth and the tracing granularity passed as keywords. What comes back is a plan — the kernel it is for, the sha256 of the cubin it was planned against, the layout of a trace record, the list of sites, and a rewriter(image, source) that will produce the patched binary. The runtime of chapter 07 calls that rewriter to get the SpliceResult it loads __init__.py:1–18. olympus trace, the benches and the verification tools name the planner by a spec, conservative | exact | search or any module:callable resolve.py:18–24. Without the first half of the promise a probe would change the kernel's outputs, and the runtime's gate would catch it. Without the second half a probe would change the kernel's timing, which is the thing being measured, and no gate catches that.

每个规划器的调用方式都一样：plan_case(case, set, …)，追踪缓冲区的环深度和追踪粒度作为关键字参数传入。返回的是一份计划——它所针对的 kernel、据以规划的那个 cubin 的 sha256、一条追踪记录的布局、站点列表，以及一个将产出补丁后二进制的 rewriter(image, source)。第 07 章的运行时调用这个重写器，得到它要加载的 SpliceResult __init__.py:1–18。olympus trace、各个 bench 和验证工具用一个 spec 指定规划器：conservative | exact | search，或任何 module:callable resolve.py:18–24。没有承诺的前一半，探针会改变 kernel 的输出，运行时的门会抓住它；没有后一半，探针会改变 kernel 的时序，而时序正是被测量的东西，没有任何门能抓住它。

§2Invariant不变量

Invariant不变量3

A probe never waits on a scoreboard only the kernel claims. Such a wait would turn the kernel's overlapped latency into a stall and change what is being measured. A site with no free scoreboard is refused, never reclaimed.

探针从不等待只有 kernel 占用的 scoreboard。这样的等待会把 kernel 本可重叠的延迟变成停顿，改变被测量的东西。没有空闲 scoreboard 的站点直接拒绝，绝不回收。

Checked by a static check over every wait bit of every probe instruction: each must be justified by a claim the probe made earlier in its island, or by a register it touches that the kernel has pending exact.py:1108–1112; by the rewriter's hazard check of the real spliced cubin, which fails closed; and by the scoreboard scan of the final run, which finds no island waiting on a kernel scoreboard (EVAL.md). The refusal is invariant 3's: a site with no free scoreboard raises ScheduleError before any launch exact.py:90–91; 10 reviewed corpus sites and about 1% of fuzz-generated sites are refused this way (EVAL.md).

检查方式：一个静态检查遍历每条探针指令的每个等待位，每一位都必须有依据：要么是探针在同一 island 中更早登记的占用，要么是它触及的、kernel 仍挂在该 scoreboard 上的寄存器 exact.py:1108–1112；重写器对真实拼接后 cubin 的冒险检查，失败即停；以及最终运行的 scoreboard 扫描，没有发现任何 island 等待 kernel 的 scoreboard（EVAL.md）。拒绝属于不变量 3：没有空闲 scoreboard 的站点在任何启动之前抛出 ScheduleError exact.py:90–91；语料库中 10 个审阅过的站点和约 1% 的模糊测试站点因此被拒绝（EVAL.md）。

The weaker rule is reclamation: when no scoreboard is free, wait early on one the kernel holds so the probe can reuse it. It costs coverage nothing and looks harmless, because the kernel would have waited on that scoreboard eventually. It is not harmless. At DeepGEMM k512's c.tile_done the kernel's TMA stores hold SB0 and the kernel waits on it only one tile later; a single spliced NOP waiting on SB0 there costs +18.4% end to end, about 0.8–0.9 µs per tile, and both the oracle tool and Olympus's own fenced planner had been paying it as a "barrier wait" in their traces (DECISIONS 61). So the rule is absolute, and the price is paid in coverage: the ten corpus sites where the kernel holds all six scoreboards are not traced (DECISIONS 63).

更弱的规则是回收：没有空闲 scoreboard 时，提前等待一个 kernel 占着的 scoreboard，好让探针复用它。它不损失覆盖，看起来也无害，因为 kernel 迟早也要等那个 scoreboard。但它并不无害。在 DeepGEMM k512 的 c.tile_done，kernel 的 TMA store 占着 SB0，而 kernel 自己要到下一个 tile 才等它；在那里只拼接一条等待 SB0 的 NOP，端到端就慢 +18.4%，每个 tile 约 0.8–0.9 µs，而 oracle 工具和 Olympus 自己带围栏的规划器都曾在追踪里把这笔开销当成"barrier 等待"记下来（DECISIONS 61）。所以这条规则是绝对的，代价用覆盖来付：语料库中 kernel 占满全部六个 scoreboard 的十个站点不被追踪（DECISIONS 63）。

§3How it works如何工作

The problem: a few instructions that have to fit问题：几条指令，要塞得下

A probe is a short run of ordinary SASS instructions — chapter 04 writes them out — and the corpus's are between 7 and 10 instructions long. Every one of them needs somewhere to put its result, and every one of them needs a control word. Those two needs are the whole of this layer's problem, and neither is free.

一个探针就是一小段普通的 SASS 指令——第 04 章把它们写了出来——语料库里的探针长 7 到 10 条指令。它们每一条都需要一个地方放结果，也每一条都需要一个控制字。这两项需求就是这一层问题的全部，而两者都不免费。

The registers must come from the dead set of chapter 02: registers whose current contents no instruction the kernel can still reach will read, because the probe is going to overwrite them. The control bits must satisfy the distances of chapter 03: enough provable cycles between each producer and each consumer, a free scoreboard for each variable-latency result, and a wait that releases every claim before the island ends. Within those two constraints there is usually a great deal of room — at the site worked through below, 160 general-purpose registers are dead and five of the six scoreboards are free — and the question is not whether a plan exists but which plan costs the kernel the fewest cycles.

寄存器必须取自第 02 章的死寄存器集合：那些当前内容不会被 kernel 任何仍可到达的指令读到的寄存器，因为探针就要覆盖它们。控制位必须满足第 03 章的距离：每个生产者与每个消费者之间有足够的可证明周期、每个可变延迟结果有一个空闲 scoreboard，以及在 island 结束前把每个占用都释放掉的等待。在这两项约束之内通常还有很大余地——在下面要走的那个站点上，有 160 个通用寄存器是死的，六个 scoreboard 里有五个空闲——所以问题不是计划存不存在，而是哪一份计划让 kernel 少花周期。

That question is a search rather than a formula, because the choices interact. Letting two of the probe's values share one register saves a register, but it also adds a WAR edge — the second writer must not land before the first reader has read — and chapter 03's ANTI table prices that edge in cycles. Reordering the probe's instructions changes which of those distances end up on the critical path, and changes which values are live at the same time, which changes what can share. Which free scoreboard a claim takes decides which single wait can close the island. Pick the registers first and you have already fixed the best order you can reach; pick the order first and you have fixed the sharing. So the three are decided together, and that joint decision is what the paper calls Algorithm 1 paper §4.2.

这个问题是一次搜索，而不是一个公式，因为几项选择互相纠缠。让探针的两个值共用一个寄存器能省下一个寄存器，但也多出一条 WAR 边——后写的那条不能在先读的那条读到之前落地——而第 03 章的 ANTI 表会以周期为这条边定价。重排探针的指令会改变这些距离中哪些落在关键路径上，也会改变哪些值同时活跃，而后者又改变哪些值能共用。某个占用取哪个空闲 scoreboard，决定了能用哪一次等待来收尾这个 island。先定寄存器，你能达到的最好顺序就已经被钉死了；先定顺序，共用方案就被钉死了。所以三者一起定，而这项联合决策就是论文所说的 Algorithm 1 论文 §4.2。

Three planners over the same templates同一套模板上的三个规划器

There are three planners because they answer to three different requirements, and each buys its answer by giving something up. The conservative planner gives up timing fidelity and owes nothing to the hazard model. The exact planner gives up optimality: it schedules one register assignment in one order. The search planner gives up nothing except planning time, which is why its planning time has to be bounded. All three produce plans that pass the same checks, so a disagreement between a conservative trace and a search trace over a kernel's outputs would point at the hazard model, not at the kernel.

之所以有三个规划器，是因为它们回答的是三种不同的要求，而每一个都要拿点东西去换。conservative 规划器放弃时序保真度，换来对冒险模型零依赖。exact 规划器放弃最优性：它只为一种寄存器分配、按一个顺序调度。search 规划器只放弃规划时间，这也正是它的规划时间必须有界的原因。三者产出的计划都要过同样的检查，所以如果 conservative 的追踪与 search 的追踪在某个 kernel 的输出上出现分歧，那指向的是冒险模型，而不是 kernel。

The conservative planner is the baseline. It places INIT before instruction 1, one RECORD before each site and an EXIT calibration before every exit, picks three persistent registers no kernel instruction touches and per-probe temporaries from the dead set, and fences every probe: a lead NOP waiting on SB0–5, 15-cycle stalls throughout, private SB0 / SB1, a trailing NOP waiting on SB0–5 again conservative.py:40–47. Its correctness depends on no latency model, which is why it exists: it is bit-exact on the whole corpus and is what olympus trace runs by default; it also perturbs timing, which is why the other two exist.

conservative 规划器是基线。它把 INIT 放在第 1 条指令之前，每个站点之前一个 RECORD，每个出口之前一个 EXIT 校准；挑三个 kernel 任何指令都不碰的持久寄存器，再从死寄存器集合里为每个探针取临时寄存器；并给每个探针加围栏：开头一条等待 SB0–5 的 NOP，全程 15 周期的 stall，私用 SB0 / SB1，结尾再一条等待 SB0–5 的 NOP conservative.py:40–47。它的正确性不依赖任何延迟模型，这正是它存在的理由：在整个语料库上逐位一致，也是 olympus trace 的默认；它同时会扰动时序，这又是另外两个存在的理由。

The exact planner takes the same plan and reschedules it hazard-exactly. Every probe instruction gets the smallest stall the hazard model of chapter 03 allows for its pairs: the latency tables and MIN_WAIT_NEEDED, or the measured minimum safe gap where the pair was measured; variable-latency results go through scoreboards drawn from the free set, every claim released before the island ends, and a wait at least SB_SET_GAP cycles after its setter exact.py:18–37. The body is list-scheduled, critical path first; schedule_island(body, ic, order) is the interface the search calls with an order of its own exact.py:806–810.

exact 规划器接过同一份计划，按冒险精确地重新调度。每条探针指令拿到第 03 章冒险模型允许的最小 stall：延迟表和 MIN_WAIT_NEEDED，或者在测量过的指令对上用实测的最小安全间隔；可变延迟的结果走 scoreboard，只从空闲集合里取，每个占用在 island 结束前释放，等待至少在设置者之后 SB_SET_GAP 个周期 exact.py:18–37。探针体按关键路径优先做列表调度；schedule_island(body, ic, order) 是搜索规划器带着自己的顺序来调用的接口 exact.py:806–810。

The search planner is Algorithm 1. Per site island it enumerates three decisions jointly: D2, the topological orders of the probe's dependencies; D1, how the probe's values share registers (the proper colourings of their interference graph, placed on the coldest dead registers); D3, which free scoreboard each claim takes. Stalls are not searched; they are derived from the same model the exact planner uses search.py:10–28. Its greedy variant prices each instruction by its critical path and issues the highest price first, in one pass search.py:55–57. It is the default of sites trace and the benches, and the planner every evidence sweep used.

search 规划器就是 Algorithm 1。对每个站点 island，它联合枚举三项决策：D2，探针依赖关系的拓扑顺序；D1，探针的值如何共用寄存器（干涉图的合法着色，放到最冷的死寄存器上）；D3，每个占用取哪个空闲 scoreboard。stall 不参与搜索，而是由与 exact 规划器相同的模型推导 search.py:10–28。它的贪心变体按关键路径给每条指令定价，一遍扫过、价高者先发 search.py:55–57。它是 sites trace 和各个 bench 的默认，也是每一次证据扫描所用的规划器。

The cost is the kernel's own delay开销就是 kernel 自己的延迟

Before anything can be chosen, the candidates have to be comparable, and the measure cannot be the island's own length. An island that is long but issues entirely inside cycles the kernel was going to spend stalled is cheap; a short island that makes the kernel wait is not. So the cost of a candidate is defined as the delay it adds to one specific instruction: the kernel's own instruction at the insertion point, the one the whole island sits in front of.

要在候选之间做选择，先得让它们可比，而这个尺度不能是 island 自身的长度。一个很长的 island，如果它的指令全都发射在 kernel 本来就要停顿的周期里，那它很便宜；一个很短的 island，如果它让 kernel 等了起来，那它不便宜。所以一个候选的开销被定义成它给某一条特定指令增加的延迟：插入点处 kernel 自己的那条指令，也就是整个 island 挡在前面的那一条。

A candidate's cost is o′ = t′[i(p)] − t0[i(p)]: the delay the island adds before the kernel's instruction at the insertion point. That is the provable island length, with every scoreboard wait priced at a measured release latency (the p50s of the measured gaps). A wait on a kernel scoreboard, allowed only for a register the probe touches that the kernel has pending, costs until that operation releases search.py:29–33. The search is depth-first with branch and bound on an admissible bound; its incumbent starts at the better of the exact planner's schedule and the greedy one, so the optimum is never worse than either search.py:35–39.

一个候选的开销是 o′ = t′[i(p)] − t0[i(p)]：island 给插入点处 kernel 指令增加的延迟。也就是可证明的 island 长度，加上每个 scoreboard 等待按实测释放延迟（实测间隔的 p50）计的价。对 kernel scoreboard 的等待，只在探针触及的寄存器仍挂在 kernel 那里时才允许，其代价一直算到那个操作释放为止 search.py:29–33。搜索是带分支定界的深度优先，下界可容许；当前最优从 exact 规划器的调度和贪心调度中较好的一个起步，所以最优解不会比两者中任何一个差 search.py:35–39。

A deterministic work budget确定性的工作量预算

The search is anytime, but it never reads the clock. Each site island gets WORK_BUDGET = 5,000 work units; every DFS node, leaf, dependency edge, kernel-context gap, renamed word and register placement is charged a calibrated number of units, about one microsecond each, so the plan is the same on every machine and in every run search.py:80–96. A site that runs out keeps its incumbent, always a valid schedule, and is reported proven: False with the phase it was capped in. Over the 2,028 sites of the final sweep the search took median 2.99 ms, p95 4.88 ms and at most 6.60 ms per site, none over the 10 ms target; 622 sites were capped, and 615 of those had already reached the optimum (DECISIONS 73).

搜索是 anytime 的，但从不读时钟。每个站点 island 有 WORK_BUDGET = 5,000 个工作量单位；每个 DFS 节点、叶子、依赖边、kernel 上下文间距查询、重命名的指令字和寄存器放置都按校准过的单位数计费，每单位约一微秒，于是计划在每台机器、每次运行上都相同 search.py:80–96。用完预算的站点保留当前最优（总是一个合法调度），报告为 proven: False 并注明在哪个阶段被截断。在最终扫描的 2,028 个站点上，每站点搜索中位数 2.99 ms、p95 4.88 ms、最长 6.60 ms，没有一个超过 10 ms 的目标；622 个站点被截断，其中 615 个其实已经到达最优（DECISIONS 73）。

Worked example: one site planned three ways实例：同一个站点，三种规划

One real site, planned by all three planners, with every number taken from the search planner's own report. It is the middle site of the widget in §5, so each step below can be replayed there. The three answers are 195, 46 and 29 cycles.

一个真实站点，三个规划器各规划一遍，每个数字都取自 search 规划器自己的报告。它就是 §5 组件里中间那个站点，所以下面每一步都能在那里重放一遍。三个答案是 195、46 和 29 个周期。

  1. The site. DeepGEMM t3_dg_1d2d_k512, memory set, insertion point instruction index 38, labelled SYNCS.EXCH.64@0x0260 — the probe records the 32-bit unsigned value that exchange is about to use. The template is 10 instructions in elected-lane form: one lane of the warp does the storing, the rest only pay the issue cycles.
  2. What the earlier chapters hand over. At index 38 the dead set holds 160 general-purpose registers, 55 uniform registers, 4 predicates and 7 uniform predicates, and the kernel holds exactly one scoreboard, SB1, leaving {0, 2, 3, 4, 5} free. Neither registers nor scoreboards are scarce here, which is what makes the site a clean look at the choices themselves.
  3. Conservative: 195 cycles. It chooses nothing. A lead NOP waits on all six scoreboards, which is what makes SB0 and SB1 safe for the probe to claim without consulting any model; the ten instructions then carry 15-cycle stalls throughout; a trailing NOP waits on all six again. 195 cycles of delay before the kernel's instruction at index 38, every one of them provably safe, and none of them chosen.
  4. Exact: 46 cycles. The same ten instructions, the same place, rescheduled with the smallest stall chapter 03 allows for each pair and with claims taken from the free set. The register assignment it is handed shares three of the probe's six values in one register — the partition is [[0,1,5],[2],[3,4]], three registers for six values. Critical-path-first list scheduling over that assignment costs 46: a 4.2× improvement on 195, from the hazard model alone.
  5. What the sharing cost. Sharing is not free, and this site is where you can see the price. Those three values in one register add four anti-dependency edges that would not otherwise exist, and they cut the probe's topological orders from 14,260 down to the 1,612 that remain legal. No order in that 1,612 costs less than 46. The exact planner was not wrong; it was not free to choose.
  6. Search: 29 cycles. The search enumerates register partitions as well as orders. Give each of the six values its own dead register — R0, R2, P3, UR2, the pair {UR0,UR1} and R3 — and the anti-edges vanish, all 14,260 orders become legal, and the cheapest of them costs 29. That is the thing neither of the other planners could reach: 29 against 46 and 195. The greedy pass found 29 too, in one sweep, by pricing each instruction at the longest chain of required distances behind it and issuing the dearest first; that answer became the search's starting incumbent, so the depth-first search only ever had to beat 29.
  7. What the budget did, and what "not proven" means. The search expanded 92 nodes and pruned 45 subtrees against its bound, took 5.06 ms, and stopped when the work counter passed 5,010 of its 5,000 units, inside the optimality phase. So the site is reported with proven: False and the phase named. Read that precisely: 29 is a complete schedule that satisfied every register and distance rule and passed the whole-program check; the admissible bound says no schedule below 28 exists; what nobody proved is that 29 is the minimum. A capped site loses a proof of optimality, never a guarantee of correctness.
  1. 这个站点。DeepGEMM t3_dg_1d2d_k512，内存集合，插入点是指令索引 38，标签 SYNCS.EXCH.64@0x0260——探针记录这次交换即将使用的那个 32 位无符号值。模板是 10 条指令的选举 lane 形式：warp 中一条 lane 负责存储，其余只付发射周期。
  2. 前面几章交过来什么。在索引 38 处，死寄存器集合里有 160 个通用寄存器、55 个 uniform 寄存器、4 个谓词和 7 个 uniform 谓词，而 kernel 只占着一个 scoreboard，即 SB1，空闲的是 {0, 2, 3, 4, 5}。这里寄存器和 scoreboard 都不紧缺，所以这个站点能干净地看清选择本身。
  3. conservative：195 个周期。它什么都不选。开头一条 NOP 等待全部六个 scoreboard，正是这一条让探针可以不查任何模型就安全地占用 SB0 和 SB1；接着那十条指令全程带 15 周期的 stall；结尾再一条 NOP 又等一遍全部六个。在索引 38 处 kernel 那条指令之前多出 195 个周期的延迟，每一个都可证明安全，也没有一个是选出来的。
  4. exact：46 个周期。同样的十条指令、同样的位置，按第 03 章为每一对允许的最小 stall 重新调度，占用从空闲集合里取。它拿到的寄存器分配把探针六个值中的三个塞进一个寄存器——分区是 [[0,1,5],[2],[3,4]]，六个值三个寄存器。在这个分配上做关键路径优先的列表调度，开销是 46：相对 195 提升 4.2 倍，而这只来自冒险模型。
  5. 共用付了什么代价。共用不是免费的，而这个站点正好能看见价钱。那三个值挤在一个寄存器里，多出四条本来不存在的反依赖边，并把探针的拓扑顺序从 14,260 个砍到只剩 1,612 个合法的。这 1,612 个里没有一个顺序能低于 46。exact 规划器没有做错；它只是没有选择的自由。
  6. search：29 个周期。搜索除了枚举顺序，也枚举寄存器分区。给六个值各自一个死寄存器——R0、R2、P3、UR2、寄存器对 {UR0,UR1} 和 R3——反依赖边随之消失，全部 14,260 个顺序都变得合法，其中最便宜的开销是 29。这是另外两个规划器都到不了的地方：29 对 46 和 195。贪心一遍也找到了 29：它按每条指令身后最长的那串必需距离给它定价，价高者先发；这个答案成了搜索的初始当前最优，于是深度优先搜索要做的只是超过 29。
  7. 预算做了什么，"未证明"又是什么意思。搜索展开了 92 个节点，靠下界剪掉了 45 棵子树，用了 5.06 ms，在工作量计数越过 5,000 个单位中的第 5,010 个时停下，当时正处在求最优的阶段。所以这个站点被报告为 proven: False，并注明阶段。这句话要照字面读：29 是一个完整的调度，满足了全部寄存器与距离规则，也通过了全程序检查；可容许的下界说明不存在低于 28 的调度；没人证明的只是 29 就是最小值。被截断的站点丢的是最优性的证明，从不是正确性的保证。

Fallbacks and refusal回退与拒绝

When the dead registers at the sites do not hold the probe, two fallbacks stand in front of the search. The default downgrades a thread-granularity plan to per-warp: the persistent state moves to three uniform registers, which survive USETMAXREG, an elected lane writes the records, and the temporaries come from the far larger dead UR set. The opt-in raises EIATTR_REGCOUNT by 8, 16 or 32 and gives the probe only the new registers, which the kernel never names; it is refused for kernels with USETMAXREG and may cost occupancy, which is why it is opt-in fallback.py:1–24. Refusal comes in two forms, both before launch: a site whose value cannot be read before the site is left out of the plan with its reason in the report (SiteRefused) conservative.py:103–107, and an island with no free scoreboard is refused by the scheduler (ScheduleError). Both are coverage gaps, never correctness risks.

当站点处的死寄存器放不下探针时，搜索前面有两道回退。默认的一道把线程粒度的计划降级为按 warp：持久状态搬到三个 uniform 寄存器（它们不受 USETMAXREG 影响），由选出的 lane 写记录，临时寄存器取自大得多的死 UR 集合。可选的一道把 EIATTR_REGCOUNT 提高 8、16 或 32，只把新增的寄存器（kernel 从未命名过）给探针；对带 USETMAXREG 的 kernel 它被拒绝，而且可能损失占用率，所以是可选项 fallback.py:1–24。拒绝有两种形式，都在启动之前：值在站点之前读不到的站点被排除在计划之外，原因写进报告（SiteRefused）conservative.py:103–107；没有空闲 scoreboard 的 island 由调度器拒绝（ScheduleError）。两者都是覆盖缺口，不是正确性风险。

"Refused" is a word worth being precise about, because it is the layer's way of never guessing. A site is refused when the rules cannot be met at it: the value the probe is supposed to record is not readable before the site — it is outside the register allocation in force there, or it is a GMMA register belonging to an in-flight wgmma group or inside a fence window, or it is the pending destination of a variable-latency instruction with no scoreboard to wait on — or else every one of the six scoreboards is claimed by the kernel, so the probe has nothing to claim. Both refusals happen on the CPU, before any launch, and both appear in the report with the site and the reason. What never happens is the third option: emitting a probe whose correctness rests on a guess. A refused site is a hole in the trace, which the report shows; it is not a risk to the kernel's output or to the timing of the sites that were planned.

"拒绝"这个词值得说准，因为它正是这一层从不靠猜的方式。当某个站点上的规则无法满足时，这个站点就被拒绝：探针本该记录的那个值在站点之前读不到——它不在该处生效的寄存器分配之内，或者它是某个仍在执行的 wgmma group 的 GMMA 寄存器、或落在某个 fence 窗口之内，或者它是一条可变延迟指令尚未完成的目的寄存器而又没有 scoreboard 可等——又或者六个 scoreboard 全被 kernel 占着，探针无可占用。两种拒绝都发生在 CPU 上、任何启动之前，并都带着站点和原因写进报告。从不发生的是第三种选项：放出一个正确性建立在猜测之上的探针。被拒绝的站点是追踪里的一个洞，报告会把它显示出来；它不是 kernel 输出的风险，也不是已规划站点时序的风险。

What the static sweep proves静态扫描证明了什么

Before a plan is trusted it is checked as a whole, on the CPU. The spliced program, the original instructions plus the islands on the original control flow, goes through the hazard model's check; a violation that touches a probe is repaired, a longer stall or one more wait, and the check repeated until clean exact.py:908–911. The rewriter then checks the real spliced cubin again, decoded with its own control flow, and fails closed. The static sweep runs this for every case × set of the corpus and adds, per planner, the text check of every island word against cuobjdump, sass_depcheck on the spliced kernel against the original, binary similarity (A2), REGCOUNT unchanged (A3), and the per-site report of cost, proof or cap, registers and search time (A8) sweep.py:1–28.

一份计划在被信任之前，要在 CPU 上作为整体检查。拼接后的程序，即原指令加上原控制流上的各个 island，过一遍冒险模型的 check；涉及探针的违例会被修复（更长的 stall，或多一个等待），然后重复检查直到干净 exact.py:908–911。随后重写器用真实拼接后的 cubin 再查一遍，按它自己解码出的控制流，失败即停。静态扫描对语料库的每个用例 × 集合都这样做，并按规划器加上：每个 island 指令字与 cuobjdump 的文本比对、拼接后 kernel 对照原 kernel 的 sass_depcheck、二进制相似度（A2）、REGCOUNT 不变（A3），以及每站点的开销、证明或截断、寄存器与搜索时间报告（A8）sweep.py:1–28。

§4Where it came from从何而来

The paper turns splicing into a search paper §4.2. It defines the overhead of a splicing as the delay it adds to the kernel's instruction at the insertion address, and it computes that delay by giving each instruction an issue time: the latest of its predecessors' issue times, plus the spacing required between the two. Dynamic dependencies, which have no required spacing, are priced at measured latencies instead. Three decisions are then enumerated jointly — the instruction order, the register assignment and the control bits — and the cheapest combination is kept. A greedy variant skips the enumeration: it prices each instruction by its critical path (Eq. 4) and issues the highest price first. Olympus's D1, D2, D3, its cost and its greedy are those. The two register-shortage remedies, downgrading thread-level tracing to per-warp and raising the register count, are the paper's fallbacks paper §4.3. The paper also reports the search's cost in time per site paper §5.6; Olympus set itself the same kind of target, at most 10 ms per site, and met it.

论文把拼接变成一个搜索问题 论文 §4.2。它把一次拼接的开销定义为它给插入地址处 kernel 指令增加的延迟，并通过给每条指令一个发射时间来算出这段延迟：取它各前驱发射时间中最晚的那个，加上两者之间所需的间距。动态依赖没有所需间距，改按实测延迟计价。随后三项决策被联合枚举——指令顺序、寄存器分配和控制位——保留最便宜的组合。贪心变体跳过枚举：它按关键路径给每条指令定价（式 4），价高者先发。Olympus 的 D1、D2、D3、开销定义和贪心都来自这里。两种寄存器不足时的补救，把线程级追踪降为按 warp，以及提高寄存器数，是论文的回退 论文 §4.3。论文还以每站点时间报告搜索的代价 论文 §5.6；Olympus 给自己定了同类目标，每站点最多 10 ms，并达到了它。

Where Olympus departs: a work budget, not a clockOlympus 的不同之处：工作量预算，而不是时钟DECISIONS 73

A search bounded by wall-clock time gives a different plan on a different machine, or on the same machine on a busy day, and the spliced image changes with it. Olympus bounds the search by counted work: 5,000 units per site, each operation charged a calibrated cost, so the plan is a pure function of the cubin and the sites. The calibration keeps a unit near one microsecond, which is how the budget stays under 10 ms per site while never reading the clock DECISIONS.md:364–369.

以墙钟时间为界的搜索，换一台机器，或者同一台机器赶上忙的一天，就会给出不同的计划，拼接后的镜像也随之改变。Olympus 用计数的工作量为界：每站点 5,000 个单位，每种操作按校准过的代价计费，于是计划是 cubin 和站点的纯函数。校准让一个单位接近一微秒，这就是预算既能把每站点控制在 10 ms 以内、又从不读时钟的原因 DECISIONS.md:364–369。

Where Olympus departs: no reclamationOlympus 的不同之处：不回收DECISIONS 63

The paper's reference tool reaches a site with no free scoreboard by waiting on one the kernel holds; that is how it traces the ten corpus sites Olympus does not (EVAL.md). Olympus draws scoreboards only from the free set, waits on a kernel scoreboard only for a register the probe itself touches, and refuses the site otherwise; the first hazard-exact scheduler had reclaimed in 117 islands and reproduced exactly the artifact of DECISIONS 61, so reclamation was dropped DECISIONS.md:227–232.

论文的参考工具靠等待 kernel 占着的 scoreboard 来到达没有空闲 scoreboard 的站点；语料库里 Olympus 不追踪而它追踪的十个站点就是这么来的（EVAL.md）。Olympus 只从空闲集合取 scoreboard，只在探针自己触及的寄存器挂在 kernel scoreboard 上时才等它，否则拒绝该站点；第一版按冒险精确调度的调度器曾在 117 个 island 里做过回收，并恰好复现了 DECISIONS 61 的伪影，于是回收被放弃 DECISIONS.md:227–232。

Beyond the paper: three planners and a sweep论文之外：三个规划器和一次扫描

The fenced planner, the hazard-exact list scheduler and the static sweep are Olympus's. The paper describes the fenced approach only as the naive splicer it improves on; Olympus built it first, on purpose, as the correctness baseline the other two are measured against, and kept it as the olympus trace default. On the final pass the greedy order equals the optimal one on 1,603 of 1,644 T1–T4 sites; all 31 worse sites use a borrowed or saved predicate (DECISIONS 75).

带围栏的规划器、按冒险精确的列表调度器和静态扫描是 Olympus 自己的。论文只把围栏方法当作它所改进的朴素拼接器来描述；Olympus 有意先把它做出来，作为另外两个规划器据以衡量的正确性基线，并保留为 olympus trace 的默认。在最终一轮上，贪心顺序在 1,644 个 T1–T4 站点中的 1,603 个上与最优相同；差的 31 个站点都用了借来的或保存过的谓词（DECISIONS 75）。
