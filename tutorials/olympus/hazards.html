Not getting in the way不碍事

A GPU core interlocks nothing. Two dependent instructions issue the right distance apart because the compiler encoded that distance into their control bits, and a variable-latency result is waited for because the compiler named a scoreboard. Once a probe is spliced in, those are the planner's responsibilities, and the hazard model is what it knows. This chapter is the model, where its numbers come from, how it proves itself on ptxas's own code, and the three rules it learned on the GPU.

GPU 核心不做任何互锁。两条相互依赖的指令之所以隔着正确的距离发射，是因为编译器把这段距离编进了它们的控制位；一个可变延迟的结果之所以被等到，是因为编译器指定了一个 scoreboard。探针一旦拼进去，这些就成了规划器的责任，而冒险模型就是它所知道的一切。本章讲这个模型、它的数字从哪来、它如何在 ptxas 自己的代码上证明自己，以及它在 GPU 上学到的三条规则。

§1Contract契约

What this layer promises这一层的承诺

The minimum issue distance and the scoreboard rules that keep a probe from stalling the kernel.

让探针不会使 kernel 停顿的最小发射间距和 scoreboard 规则。

olympus/hazards → used by使用者 olympus/planner (the exact and search schedulers) and（exact 与 search 调度器）和 olympus/verify · reads读取 olympus/isa (decode, the control word) and（解码、控制字）和 olympus/cfg (paths)（路径）

The planners (chapter 04) ask this layer two questions for every pair of instructions that involves a probe: how many cycles must separate them, and if the producer's latency is not fixed, which scoreboard must the consumer wait on. They then hand the whole spliced program back for check, and the rewriter checks the real spliced cubin once more before the runtime (chapter 02) may load it. The promise is two-sided. Too small a distance and the probe reads a stale register or corrupts one the kernel still needs; too large, or a wait on the wrong scoreboard, and the probe stalls the warp, which is the one thing a tracer must not do to the thing it measures.

规划器（第 04 章）对每一对涉及探针的指令向这一层问两个问题：它们之间必须隔多少周期；如果生产者的延迟不固定，消费者必须等哪个 scoreboard。然后它们把整个拼接后的程序交回来 check，重写器再对真实拼接后的 cubin 查一遍，运行时（第 02 章）才可以加载它。这个承诺是双向的：间距太小，探针会读到过期的寄存器，或者破坏 kernel 还需要的寄存器；间距太大，或者等错了 scoreboard，探针就会让 warp 停顿，而这是追踪器对被测对象最不该做的事。

§2Invariant不变量

Invariants不变量3 · 4

A probe never waits on a scoreboard only the kernel claims; such a wait would turn the kernel's overlapped latency into a stall and change what is being measured. A wait is issued at least 2 cycles after the instruction that sets its scoreboard, the rule ptxas itself follows in every kernel we have examined.

探针从不等待只有 kernel 占用的 scoreboard；这样的等待会把 kernel 本可重叠的延迟变成停顿，改变被测量的东西。等待至少在设置其 scoreboard 的指令之后 2 个周期发出，这是 ptxas 在我们检查过的每个 kernel 中都遵守的规则。

Checked by the hazard model's check over the spliced program and again over the real spliced cubin, which reports every probe store without a read scoreboard and every probe variable-latency write without a write scoreboard model.py:488–491; by the planner's static check of every probe wait bit (chapter 04); by SB-SET, which flags any wait fewer than SB_SET_GAP = 2 cycles after its setter exact.py:79–86; and by the self-check's sbgap survey, which is the rule's evidence: over 976 ptxas kernels, 0 waits at distance 1 against 5,314 write and 1,416 read claims waited on at distance 2 (DECISIONS 62).

检查方式：冒险模型的 check 先过拼接后的程序，再过真实拼接后的 cubin，报告每一个没有读 scoreboard 的探针 store 和每一个没有写 scoreboard 的探针可变延迟写入 model.py:488–491；规划器对每个探针等待位的静态检查（第 04 章）；SB-SET，标出任何距离设置者不足 SB_SET_GAP = 2 个周期的等待 exact.py:79–86；以及自检的 sbgap 普查，它是这条规则的依据：976 个 ptxas kernel 中，距离为 1 的等待 0 个，而在距离 2 处被等待的写占用有 5,314 个、读占用 1,416 个（DECISIONS 62）。

Both rules replaced something weaker that the documentation allowed. The latency file's TABLE_TRUE(SCOREBOARD) says ORDERED_ZERO: a wait may follow its setter immediately. The first hazard-exact scheduler believed it, waited one cycle after an S2R that claimed SB0, read a stale register, and 0 of 57 trace-buffer checks passed on the GPU. And a weaker form of invariant 3, "wait on a kernel scoreboard when it is cheap", needs a latency the model does not have: how long an asynchronous TMA store or bulk copy will hold its scoreboard. At k512's c.tile_done the answer was about 1,450 cycles; the widget below shows what that does to one warp.

两条规则各自取代了一个文档允许、却更弱的说法。延迟文件的 TABLE_TRUE(SCOREBOARD) 写的是 ORDERED_ZERO：等待可以紧跟在设置者之后。第一版按冒险精确调度的调度器相信了它，在占用 SB0 的 S2R 之后一个周期就等待，读到了过期的寄存器，GPU 上 57 次追踪缓冲区检查 0 次通过。而不变量 3 的弱化版"便宜的时候就等 kernel 的 scoreboard"，需要一个模型没有的延迟：一次异步的 TMA store 或 bulk copy 会把它的 scoreboard 占多久。在 k512 的 c.tile_done，答案是约 1,450 个周期；下面的组件展示这对一个 warp 意味着什么。

§3How it works如何工作

Fixed latency, variable latency固定延迟，可变延迟

An in-order warp issues instruction i+1 at least gap(i) cycles after i, where the gap is the stall count in the control word, and at least 1. For a fixed-latency pair the model sums the gaps along every path from producer to consumer and requires the provable delay to reach the table value: TABLE_TRUE for a read after a write, TABLE_OUTPUT for a write after a write, TABLE_ANTI for a write after a read, plus pipe occupancy. A variable-latency result (a load, an S2R, a store's late read of its sources) has no table value at all: it is pending from issue until some later instruction waits on its scoreboard, and every later access to the register must be preceded, on every path, by such a wait. A third pass handles the asynchronous WGMMA groups model.py:1–30.

一个按序发射的 warp 在指令 i 之后至少 gap(i) 个周期才发射 i+1，这个间隔就是控制字里的 stall 计数，且至少为 1。对固定延迟的指令对，模型沿生产者到消费者的每一条路径把间隔累加，要求可证明的延迟达到表值：写后读查 TABLE_TRUE，写后写查 TABLE_OUTPUT，读后写查 TABLE_ANTI，再加流水线占用。可变延迟的结果（一次 load、一条 S2R、store 对其源操作数的延后读取）根本没有表值：它从发射起一直悬而未决，直到后面某条指令等待它的 scoreboard，而之后对该寄存器的每次访问，在每条路径上都必须先有这样一次等待。第三遍处理异步的 WGMMA group model.py:1–30。

Six scoreboards, set and waited on六个 scoreboard：登记与等待

Each instruction can set one write scoreboard (wr_sb: my result is pending) and one read scoreboard (rd_sb: I will read my GPR sources later, so do not overwrite them yet), and can wait on any subset through its 6-bit wait mask or a DEPBAR.LE. A scoreboard with no outstanding claim is free model.py:1093–1098. Probes are held to a stricter standard than the kernel: a probe store must set a read scoreboard (no fixed gap is safe for store sources; they are stale for up to 32 cycles without one) and a probe variable-latency write must set a write scoreboard, because probes never lean on the in-order-queue economies ptxas uses; the checker reports SB-NORD and SB-NOWR otherwise measured.py:20–24.

每条指令可以设置一个写 scoreboard（wr_sb：我的结果尚未就绪）和一个读 scoreboard（rd_sb：我稍后才读我的 GPR 源操作数，先别覆盖它们），并通过 6 位等待掩码或一条 DEPBAR.LE 等待任意子集。没有未决占用的 scoreboard 是空闲的 model.py:1093–1098。探针被要求得比 kernel 更严：探针的 store 必须设置读 scoreboard（store 的源操作数没有任何安全的固定间隔；不等它们会有长达 32 个周期的过期窗口），探针的可变延迟写入必须设置写 scoreboard，因为探针从不依赖 ptxas 所用的按序队列省略；否则检查器报告 SB-NORD 和 SB-NOWR measured.py:20–24。

Where the numbers come from数字从哪里来

The tables are nvidia-sass-document's sm_90_latencies.txt (and sm_103_latencies.txt), parsed completely by Olympus's own parser: operation sets with their set algebra, connector roles, the TRUE / OUTPUT / ANTI tables per register file, the occupancy table; the two known bugs of the upstream parser cannot occur, because table bodies are cut out before any set is read and statements run to their terminating semicolon tables.py:1–36. Each variable-latency class's execution queue comes from the instruction dump, scanned line by line isaprops.py:1–11. ptxas itself schedules some pairs closer than the table, so for the kernel's pairs the model accepts a gap at or above ptxas's floor for that exact table cell: the smallest gap ptxas was observed to use over 957 ptxas kernels outside the corpus, each floor with its mechanism (integer forwarding, late operand reads, back-to-back FMA issue, a blocking barrier) relaxations.py:1–8 (DECISIONS 43). Probe pairs never take the floors. They may take only the measured minimum safe gaps: pairs the probes use, measured on H200 with every thread fresh at every gap up to the table value, across filler shapes, alignments and launch shapes, up to 330 M threads per pair; an unmatched pair keeps the table measured.py:1–18 (DECISIONS 46). One floor is the exception: a probe directly after a blocking BAR.SYNC may take ptxas's floor of 1 for that cell, since the barrier itself holds the warp (DECISIONS 50).

表来自 nvidia-sass-document 的 sm_90_latencies.txt（以及 sm_103_latencies.txt），由 Olympus 自己的解析器完整解析：带集合代数的操作集合、连接器角色、按寄存器文件的 TRUE / OUTPUT / ANTI 表、占用表；上游解析器的两个已知 bug 在这里不可能发生，因为表体在读任何集合之前就被切出，而语句一直读到结尾的分号 tables.py:1–36。每个可变延迟指令类的执行队列来自指令转储，逐行扫描 isaprops.py:1–11。ptxas 自己有些指令对排得比表更紧，所以对 kernel 的指令对，模型接受不低于该表格单元 ptxas 下限的间隔：在语料库之外 957 个 ptxas kernel 上观察到 ptxas 用过的最小间隔，每个下限都附有机制（整数前递、延后的操作数读取、背靠背的 FMA 发射、阻塞式 barrier）relaxations.py:1–8（DECISIONS 43）。探针的指令对从不使用下限。它们只能使用实测的最小安全间隔：探针用到的指令对，在 H200 上测量，从该间隔到表值之间的每个间隔上每个线程都读到新值，覆盖各种填充形状、对齐和启动形状，每对最多 3.3 亿线程；没有匹配到的指令对仍用表值 measured.py:1–18（DECISIONS 46）。有一个下限是例外：紧跟阻塞式 BAR.SYNC 之后的探针可以用 ptxas 对该单元的下限 1，因为 barrier 本身就把 warp 挡住了（DECISIONS 50）。

The self-check: ptxas's code must pass自检：ptxas 的代码必须通过

A hazard model that rejects correct code is wrong, so the model is run over every original corpus kernel along its control flow: fixed latencies and occupancy against the table with the floors applied, scoreboards and WGMMA groups exactly. The result on all 19 corpus kernels is 0 unexplained violations and 0 scoreboard or GMMA violations; the 7,245 original pairs below the table are all inside a floor, and the free scoreboards the model computes agree with the liveness analysis at all 19,858 reachable instructions (DECISIONS 43). The same tool calibrates the floors on kernels that are not in the corpus, checks held-out cubin lists, and runs sbgap, the setter-to-wait distance survey behind invariant 4 selfcheck.py:1–31. The walk over a kernel's instructions and successors is shared with the planners, so they check the same graph the self-check proved walk.py:1–4.

一个会拒绝正确代码的冒险模型是错的，所以模型会沿控制流过一遍语料库里的每个原始 kernel：固定延迟和占用对照应用了下限的表，scoreboard 和 WGMMA group 精确检查。在全部 19 个语料库 kernel 上的结果是 0 个无法解释的违例、0 个 scoreboard 或 GMMA 违例；低于表值的 7,245 对原始指令全部在某个下限之内，模型算出的空闲 scoreboard 在全部 19,858 条可达指令上与活跃性分析一致（DECISIONS 43）。同一个工具在语料库之外的 kernel 上校准下限、检查保留的 cubin 列表，并运行 sbgap，即不变量 4 背后的设置者到等待距离普查 selfcheck.py:1–31。遍历 kernel 指令及其后继的代码与规划器共用，所以它们检查的正是自检证明过的那张图 walk.py:1–4。

Three rules learned on the GPU在 GPU 上学到的三条规则

  1. Never wait on a scoreboard only the kernel claims. At each tile end of DeepGEMM k512 the TMA-store leader warps commit stores that set SB0, and the kernel waits on it only one tile later. Both the oracle tool and the fenced planner waited on SB0 at c.tile_done, and both traces showed a ~1.1–1.3 µs "barrier wait" per tile that was not there without probes. One spliced NOP waiting on SB0 costs +18.4% end to end (DECISIONS 61). Implemented as: scoreboards only from the free set; a kernel scoreboard waited on only for a register the probe touches; no free scoreboard means the site is refused, never reclaimed (DECISIONS 63).
  2. A wait comes at least 2 cycles after its setter. The table says a scoreboard may be waited on at distance zero; ptxas never does it. Over 976 kernels there are 0 set-to-wait pairs at distance 1, against 5,314 write and 1,416 read claims waited on at distance 2, apart from after a draining USETMAXREG. The one scheduler that waited at distance 1 read stale slot headers and addresses: 0 of 57 buffer checks passed. SB_SET_GAP = 2, checked as SB-SET in the repair loop and in the rewriter (DECISIONS 62).
  3. Never DRAIN. The first null islands used ptxas's padding word NOP ;[7:7:{}:0:1], whose usched 0 drains the warp's pipeline before the next issue. Before every block leader it cost +59.6% on matmul, +29% on FlashMLA and +26% on k512; the same splice with a stall-1 word costs +0.1% / +1.1% / +0.9%. The island filler is now ptxas's stall extender @!UPT UIADD3 URZ, …, which never executes and claims nothing islands.py:40–45, and the schedulers reject stall 0 (DECISIONS 54).
  1. 从不等待只有 kernel 占用的 scoreboard。在 DeepGEMM k512 每个 tile 结尾，负责 TMA store 的 leader warp 提交 store 并设置 SB0，而 kernel 要到下一个 tile 才等它。oracle 工具和带围栏的规划器都在 c.tile_done 等了 SB0，两者的追踪里都出现了每 tile 约 1.1–1.3 µs 的"barrier 等待"，没有探针时它并不存在。只拼接一条等待 SB0 的 NOP，端到端就慢 +18.4%（DECISIONS 61）。实现为：scoreboard 只从空闲集合取；只在探针触及的寄存器挂在某个 kernel scoreboard 上时才等它；没有空闲 scoreboard 就拒绝该站点，绝不回收（DECISIONS 63）。
  2. 等待至少在设置者之后 2 个周期。表说 scoreboard 可以在距离零处被等待；ptxas 从不这么做。976 个 kernel 中，距离为 1 的登记到等待对有 0 个，而距离为 2 处被等待的写占用有 5,314 个、读占用 1,416 个，唯一的例外在排空流水线的 USETMAXREG 之后。唯一一个在距离 1 处等待的调度器读到了过期的槽位头和地址：57 次缓冲区检查 0 次通过。SB_SET_GAP = 2，在修复循环和重写器中以 SB-SET 检查（DECISIONS 62）。
  3. 永远不用 DRAIN。最早的空 island 用了 ptxas 的填充字 NOP ;[7:7:{}:0:1]，它的 usched 0 会在下一次发射前排空 warp 的流水线。放在每个基本块首指令之前，它让 matmul 慢 +59.6%、FlashMLA 慢 +29%、k512 慢 +26%；同样的拼接换成 stall 为 1 的字，只慢 +0.1% / +1.1% / +0.9%。island 的填充字现在是 ptxas 的 stall 延长器 @!UPT UIADD3 URZ, …，它永不执行、不占用任何东西 islands.py:40–45，而调度器拒绝 stall 0（DECISIONS 54）。

§4Where it came from从何而来

The paper's model starts from how a GPU core issues: in order, one warp instruction at a time, every hazard resolved by the compiler and encoded in the control bits, fixed-latency dependencies by issue distance and variable-latency ones by scoreboards paper §4.1. A spliced probe inherits that contract, so the splicer resolves the probe's hazards with the same tables, attaches a scoreboard to each dynamic dependency from the free set, and, when none is free, reclaims a busy one by waiting on it early paper §4.4. The evaluation counts the dependency waits each tool's probes add and shows the paper's tool keeping only the unavoidable RAW wait paper §5.4.3. Olympus keeps the model and holds itself to that count; the three departures below are where the GPU disagreed with the documentation the model is built on.

论文的模型从 GPU 核心如何发射讲起：按序，每次一条 warp 指令，所有冒险由编译器解决并编进控制位，固定延迟的依赖靠发射间距，可变延迟的依赖靠 scoreboard 论文 §4.1。拼进去的探针继承这份约定，所以拼接器用同一套表解决探针的冒险，从空闲集合里给每个动态依赖分配一个 scoreboard，没有空闲的就提前等待一个忙碌的、把它回收过来 论文 §4.4。评估部分统计各工具的探针额外引入的依赖等待，并展示论文的工具只保留不可避免的 RAW 等待 论文 §5.4.3。Olympus 保留了这个模型，并以同样的计数要求自己；下面三处不同，都是 GPU 与模型所依据的文档意见相左的地方。

Where Olympus departs: no early waits on the kernel's scoreboardsOlympus 的不同之处：不提前等待 kernel 的 scoreboardDECISIONS 61

The paper's §4.4 reclaims a busy scoreboard by an early wait, and its tool does so at k512's c.tile_done. Olympus measured that wait alone: +18.4% [18.25, 18.61] at k512, +5.3% at k2048, against −0.3% for the same island without it; it explained 18.4 of the oracle's 27.3 overhead points on that kernel, and the "barrier wait" both tools had reported was this artifact DECISIONS.md:215–221. The rule became invariant 3.

论文 §4.4 用提前等待来回收忙碌的 scoreboard，它的工具在 k512 的 c.tile_done 就是这么做的。Olympus 单独测了这一次等待：k512 上 +18.4% [18.25, 18.61]，k2048 上 +5.3%，而同一个 island 不带它是 −0.3%；它解释了 oracle 在该 kernel 上 27.3 个百分点开销中的 18.4 个，两个工具都报告过的"barrier 等待"就是这个伪影 DECISIONS.md:215–221。这条规则成了不变量 3。

Where Olympus departs: 2 cycles between a setter and its waitOlympus 的不同之处：设置者与等待之间隔 2 个周期DECISIONS 62

The latency file allows a wait at distance zero and the paper does not say otherwise. ptxas, surveyed over 976 kernels, never waits at distance 1, and the scheduler that did wrote stale slot headers on the GPU. Olympus requires 2 provable cycles between a setter and any wait on its scoreboard, kernel setters just before an island included DECISIONS.md:222–226. The rule became invariant 4.

延迟文件允许在距离零处等待，论文也没有另作说明。在 976 个 kernel 上普查，ptxas 从不在距离 1 处等待，而那个这么做的调度器在 GPU 上写出了过期的槽位头。Olympus 要求设置者与任何对其 scoreboard 的等待之间有 2 个可证明的周期，紧邻 island 之前的 kernel 设置者也算在内 DECISIONS.md:222–226。这条规则成了不变量 4。

Where Olympus departs: refuse, do not reclaimOlympus 的不同之处：拒绝，而不回收DECISIONS 63

With no free scoreboard the paper's splicer reclaims; Olympus refuses the site and reports it as a coverage gap. The first hazard-exact scheduler had reclaimed in 117 islands, 66 of them in FlashMLA's memory set, and reproduced the artifact above; the exception for registers the probe itself touches is the only wait on a kernel scoreboard that remains DECISIONS.md:227–232.
