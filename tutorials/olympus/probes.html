What a probe is探针是什么

A probe is a few SASS instructions that read a clock, pack a word and store it. The templates write those instructions for one architecture with every control bit neutral, and they never pick a register: the planner hands them dead ones. What the probes write is record format v1, a per-warp slot whose decoder can rebuild every timestamp from the slot's own two clock anchors, so the result does not depend on any assumed frequency. Both halves are proven on the GPU before a splicer or a runtime ever touches them.

探针就是几条 SASS 指令：读一个时钟，打包成一个字，写出去。模板为一种架构写出这几条指令，所有控制位保持中性，并且从不自己挑寄存器：规划器把死寄存器交给它。探针写下的是记录格式 v1，每个 warp 一个槽位，解码器能凭槽位自己的两个时钟锚点重建每一个时间戳，所以结果不依赖任何假设的频率。这两半在拼接器或运行时碰到它们之前，就先在 GPU 上证明过。

§1Contract契约

What this layer promises这一层的承诺

A template body is correct SASS for its arch with neutral control bits, ready to be scheduled. Probes and decoders agree on every bit of record format v1: per-warp slots, a 32-byte header, word0 = (clk << id_bits) | (site_id + 1), integer-ns calibration.

模板主体是对应架构上正确的 SASS，控制位中性，等待被调度。探针与解码器对记录格式 v1 的每一位达成一致：每 warp 一个槽位、32 字节头部、word0 = (clk << id_bits) | (site_id + 1)、整数纳秒校准。

olympus/probes → used by使用者 olympus/planner · olympus/trace/record.py → shared by共用于 olympus/probes and与 olympus/trace/decode.py

Two promises, and both of them are about bits. The first is about instruction bits. A template body is correct SASS for one architecture: every instruction in it encodes to a word and decodes back to its own text, which the tests check on both arches. Its control bits are neutral, which means the body presumes nothing about where it will sit — stall 15, no scoreboard claimed, no wait on anything — so whoever schedules it is free to tighten it without first undoing an assumption templates.py:243–255. The second promise is about record bits: the templates and the decoder take record format v1 out of the same module, so the bytes one writes are the bytes the other expects, field for field.

两条承诺，都关于比特。第一条关于指令的比特。模板主体是某一种构架上正确的 SASS：其中每一条指令都能编码成一个字、再解码回它自己的文本，测试在两种构架上都核对过这一点。它的控制位是中性的，即主体对自己将要坐在哪里不做任何预设——stall 15、不占用 scoreboard、不等待任何东西——所以谁来调度它，都可以直接收紧，而不用先推翻一个假设 templates.py:243–255。第二条承诺关于记录的比特：模板与解码器从同一个模块里取用记录格式 v1，所以一方写下的字节就是另一方期待的字节，字段对字段。

Each promise has one consumer. The planner, which chapter 05 is about, builds every island out of these bodies: it asks a template which registers it needs (needs_init, needs_record, needs_exit), hands over dead ones in exactly that shape, and then schedules the body by choosing its control bits. The decoder, which chapter 08 is about, reads back what those bodies wrote into device memory. record.py is one of the four contract modules any layer may import, which is why the templates and the decoder share a single definition of a slot rather than keeping two copies of it in step record.py:34–78.

每条承诺各有一个使用者。规划器（第 05 章的主题）用这些主体构造每一个 island：它先问模板需要哪些寄存器（needs_init、needs_record、needs_exit），按这个形状交给它死寄存器，然后通过选择控制位来调度主体。解码器（第 08 章的主题）把这些主体写进设备内存的东西读回来。record.py 是任何层都可以导入的四个契约模块之一，这就是模板和解码器共用同一份槽位定义、而不是让两份拷贝保持同步的原因 record.py:34–78。

Without the first promise the planner’s search would have nothing sound to permute: a body that already assumed a schedule could not be rescheduled safely. Without the second, a trace would decode into the wrong sites at the wrong times while every one of the kernel’s outputs stayed bit-exact — the one kind of failure no correctness gate can catch.

没有第一条承诺，规划器的搜索就没有可靠的东西可以排列：一个已经预设了某种调度的主体，无法被安全地重新调度。没有第二条，一份追踪会在 kernel 输出逼位一致的同时，把错的站点解到错的时间上——这是唯一一种正确性门捕捉不到的故障。

§2Invariant不变量

Invariant不变量2

A probe uses only registers proven dead, including registers held by in-flight asynchronous operations until their release point.

探针只使用被证明已死的寄存器，包括被进行中的异步操作占用到其释放点为止的寄存器。

Checked by the templates themselves, which take registers only through Persistent and Temps, verify the shape, the alignment and the arch's register range, and refuse any overlap between persistent, temporary and recorded registers templates.py:129–147; by the dead-register check, which overwrites every register the analysis calls dead and requires bit-identical outputs, on every corpus kernel on both arches; and, for the templates' own correctness, by the standalone GPU proof of invariant 5: 11 of 11 configurations on 132 SMs, 0 stray bytes, outputs identical to the unprobed kernel (DECISIONS 40).

检查方式：模板自身只通过 Persistent 与 Temps 接收寄存器，核对形状、对齐和该架构的寄存器范围，并拒绝持久、临时与被记录寄存器之间的任何重叠 templates.py:129–147；死寄存器检查改写分析判定为死的每一个寄存器，并要求输出逐位一致，在两种架构的每个语料库 kernel 上通过；至于模板自身的正确性，由不变量 5 的独立 GPU 证明保证：11 个配置全部通过，132 个 SM，0 个多余字节，输出与无探针的 kernel 相同（DECISIONS 40）。

A weaker rule would let a template pick "a register that looks free", for instance one the kernel does not name nearby. The templates cannot know liveness, and they do not try: the proof that a register is dead lives in one place, the liveness analysis, and the planner carries it to the template as a handed-over register. That is why every body builder takes st: Persistent and t: Temps and raises on anything that is not the shape it asked for; a template cannot be made to write a register it was not given templates.py:1–7.

更弱的规则会允许模板挑一个"看起来空闲"的寄存器，比如 kernel 在附近没有点名的那个。模板不可能知道活跃性，也不去尝试：寄存器已死的证明只在一处，即活跃性分析，由规划器以"交付的寄存器"这一形式带到模板。所以每个主体构造函数都接收 st: Persistent 和 t: Temps，凡是不符合它所要求形状的一律抛错；没有人能让模板去写一个没有交给它的寄存器 templates.py:1–7。

“Dead” is chapter 02’s word, and its promise is exact: a register the liveness analysis calls dead at an instruction can be overwritten there without changing the kernel’s outputs. The handover of such registers has a shape. needs_init, needs_record and needs_exit each return a Needs: how many GPRs, how many uniform registers and how many predicates, the first two as tuples of group sizes, where 1 is a single register, 2 an even-aligned pair and 4 a quad aligned to four. The planner allocates dead registers into exactly those groups and passes them back as a Temps, in the same order templates.py:166–210. The template then checks what it was handed: the groups match what it asked for, the pairs are even, the quads are aligned, no register is above this arch’s top (UR62 on sm_90, UR79 on sm_103, and one lower inside a {..} register group), and no register is shared between the persistent state, the temporaries and a register being recorded templates.py:337–364.

“死”是第 02 章的用词，它的承诺很精确：活跃性分析在某条指令处判定为死的寄存器，在那里被改写不会改变 kernel 的输出。这类寄存器的交付有一个形状。needs_init、needs_record、needs_exit 各返回一个 Needs：要多少个 GPR、多少个 uniform 寄存器、多少个谓词，前两者以组大小的元组给出，其中 1 是单个寄存器、2 是按偶对齐的一对、4 是按四对齐的四元组。规划器把死寄存器按这些组分配好，再以同样的顺序做成一个 Temps 交回来 templates.py:166–210。模板随后核对自己拿到的东西：组与它所要的一致、成对的是偶数、四元组是对齐的、没有寄存器超过该构架的上限（sm_90 上是 UR62，sm_103 上是 UR79，而在 {..} 寄存器组内还要低一个），且持久状态、临时寄存器和被记录的寄存器之间没有任何共用 templates.py:337–364。

§3How it works如何工作

Chapter 01 showed that an instruction is a word of bits that can be taken apart and put back together unchanged. Chapter 02 showed which registers are dead at a given instruction. Chapter 03 showed how far apart two instructions must issue so that neither waits for the other. None of those three chapters wrote anything into a kernel: they established what the machine allows. This is the first chapter that writes. Everything below is bytes — the instruction bytes Olympus adds to a kernel, and the record bytes those instructions leave in memory.

第 01 章说明一条指令是一串可以拆开、再原样装回的比特。第 02 章说明在某条指令处哪些寄存器是死的。第 03 章说明两条指令之间必须隔多远发射，才能互不等待。这三章都没有往 kernel 里写下任何东西：它们确立的是机器允许什么。本章是第一个真正写东西的。下面的一切都是字节——Olympus 加进 kernel 的指令字节，以及这些指令留在内存里的记录字节。

A probe is a short run of SASS探针就是一小段 SASS

A probe has to be made of the same material as the kernel, because it is going to live inside the kernel's own instruction stream. So a probe is not a function, a callback or a trap: it is a list of SASS instructions. A template is the Python function that writes that list for one architecture, and the list it hands back is the probe's body. There are three templates, one per job. init runs once per warp and works out where that warp will write. record writes one event. exit_calib runs just before a warp leaves the kernel and writes the second half of that warp's header. A body contains no branch, no call and no loop, so it has one entry, one exit, and runs straight through templates.py:1–7.

探针必须和 kernel 用同一种材料做成，因为它要住进 kernel 自己的指令流里。所以探针不是函数、不是回调、也不是陷入：它就是一串 SASS 指令。模板是为某一种架构写出这串指令的 Python 函数，它交回来的这串指令就是探针的主体。模板有三个，各管一件事。init 每个 warp 跑一次，算出这个 warp 该往哪里写。record 写一个事件。exit_calib 在 warp 离开 kernel 之前跑，写出这个 warp 头部的后一半。主体里没有分支、没有调用、没有循环，所以只有一个入口、一个出口，一路直通 templates.py:1–7。

The bodies are small, and their sizes come from the record layout, not from the kernel. For a timestamp record with the default 6 site-id bits and a ring of 256 entries, record returns 7 instructions. exit_calib returns 5 when it is handed one aligned quad of dead GPRs. init is the long one at 33, of which 23 are the arithmetic that turns the launch geometry into this warp's slot number; it is also the only one that runs once rather than per event. Each instruction comes back as a ProbeInstr: its assembler text, its control word, and a role — one of init, capture, pack, store, advance, wait, calib — so that the planner and the hazard check can talk about what an instruction is for without parsing its text. A scheduled body becomes an island: a contiguous run of instruction words to be inserted before one chosen instruction index ir.py:78–92.

主体都很短，长度由记录布局决定，而不是由 kernel 决定。一条时间戳记录，在默认 6 个站点编号位、环深度 256 的配置下，record 返回 7 条指令。exit_calib 在拿到一组对齐的四个死 GPR 时返回 5 条。init 最长，33 条，其中 23 条是把启动几何换算成这个 warp 的槽位号的算术；它也是唯一只跑一次、而不是每个事件跑一次的。每条指令以一个 ProbeInstr 的形式回来：汇编文本、控制字，以及一个角色——init、capture、pack、store、advance、wait、calib 之一——这样规划器和冒险检查就能在不解析文本的情况下谈论一条指令是干什么的。被调度过的主体就成为一个 island：一段连续的指令字，将被插到某一条选定的指令下标之前 ir.py:78–92。

What the template does not decide模板不决定什么

Every instruction in a body comes back with the same control word, and it is deliberately the most pessimistic one the encoding can express. neutral_ctrl sets the stall count to 15, the longest delay the control word holds; sets both scoreboard fields to 7, which is the encoding for "claims none"; and sets the wait mask to 0, which is "waits for nothing". In the bracket notation of chapter 01 that reads [7:7:{}:15:1] templates.py:243–255. A body in that state is correct and slow. It cannot stall the kernel, because it claims no scoreboard the kernel might wait on; and no instruction in it can issue too early, because each one holds the next back a full 15 cycles.

主体里每一条指令回来时带的都是同一个控制字，而且是编码能表达的最悲观的那一个。neutral_ctrl 把 stall 计数设为 15，这是控制字能装下的最长延迟；把两个 scoreboard 字段都设为 7，这是"不占用任何 scoreboard"的编码；把等待掩码设为 0，即"不等待任何东西"。用第 01 章的括号记法写出来就是 [7:7:{}:15:1] templates.py:243–255。处于这个状态的主体是正确的，也是慢的。它不可能拖停 kernel，因为它没有占用任何 kernel 可能在等的 scoreboard；它里面也没有哪条指令会发射得太早，因为每条都把下一条压住整整 15 个周期。

The template stops there because the right bits are not a property of the probe. They are a property of the place it goes: what the kernel's own instructions around that index claim, how many cycles ago the kernel's previous instruction issued, which of the six scoreboards are free there. A template is given a layout and a set of registers and nothing about the surrounding code, so it cannot know any of that. Choosing the bits is the whole subject of chapter 05, and the rules that choice has to satisfy — the minimum issue distance, and never waiting on a scoreboard the kernel claims — were chapter 03's.

模板到此为止，因为正确的控制位不是探针的性质，而是它要去的那个位置的性质：kernel 自己在那个下标附近的指令占用了什么、kernel 上一条指令是几个周期前发射的、六个 scoreboard 中哪些在那里是空闲的。模板只拿到一份布局和一组寄存器，对周围的代码一无所知，所以这些它都不可能知道。选择控制位是第 05 章的全部主题，而这一选择必须满足的规则——最小发射间距，以及绝不等待 kernel 占用的 scoreboard——是第 03 章的内容。

The slot: where the bytes go槽位：字节去哪里

A probe has nowhere to put a record unless the run hands it memory, and that memory has to be divided so that no two warps ever write the same bytes. Warps on different SMs cannot coordinate cheaply, so record format v1 never asks them to: the buffer is cut into slots, one per warp, and a warp writes only its own. The buffer is n_slots slots of slot_bytes each, indexed by the linear warp id. A slot is a 32-byte header followed by a ring of ring_depth records, the whole slot padded up to a multiple of 32 bytes record.py:1–26. At the settings this chapter's example uses — 4-byte records, a ring of 256 — a slot is 32 + 256 × 4 = 1,056 bytes.

除非这次运行给探针一块内存，探针根本没地方放记录；而这块内存必须划分好，使任何两个 warp 都不会写到同样的字节。不同 SM 上的 warp 之间无法廉价协调，所以记录格式 v1 从不要求它们协调：缓冲区被切成槽位，每 warp 一个，一个 warp 只写自己的那一个。缓冲区是 n_slots 个各 slot_bytes 字节的槽位，按线性 warp 编号索引。一个槽位是 32 字节头部，之后是 ring_depth 条记录的环，整个槽位补齐到 32 字节的倍数 record.py:1–26。在本章例子所用的配置下——4 字节记录、深度 256 的环——一个槽位是 32 + 256 × 4 = 1,056 字节。

The header exists because a record alone cannot be read. It holds the two clock anchors that turn cycles into nanoseconds, the SM the warp ran on, and the number of records the warp wrote. INIT writes its first 16 bytes and EXIT the second 16, each as one store, so the three fields of each half are always consistent with each other.

头部存在的理由是：光有一条记录是读不出来的。头部装着把周期换成纳秒的两个时钟锚点、这个 warp 跑在哪个 SM 上，以及这个 warp 写了多少条记录。INIT 写前 16 字节，EXIT 写后 16 字节，各用一条 store，所以每一半里的三个字段彼此永远自洽。

A record is one word, bit by bit一条记录就是一个字，逐位解

A record must say two things — which site, and when — and it must cost one store, because the store is the part the kernel pays for. So the two things share one 32-bit word, built by one line of record.py record.py:81–91. id_bits is how many of the low bits belong to the site number. At the default 6 a set may hold 63 sites and the clock keeps the other 26 bits; raising id_bits buys sites and spends clock bits, and RecordLayout accepts 4 to 12 record.py:34–78.

一条记录必须说两件事——哪个站点、什么时候——而且必须只花一次 store，因为 store 才是 kernel 付的那部分代价。所以这两件事共用一个 32 位字，由 record.py 的一行构成 record.py:81–91。id_bits 是低位里属于站点编号的位数。默认为 6 时，一个集合最多装 63 个站点，时钟拿走另外 26 位；增大 id_bits 是用时钟位去换站点数，RecordLayout 接受 4 到 12 record.py:34–78。

word0 = (clk << id_bits) | (site_id + 1)

The + 1 is there so that an all-zero word can mean something. A site id starts at 0, so without the + 1 the word written for site 0 at cycle 0 would be indistinguishable from a word nobody wrote. With it, every written record is non-zero, and an entry that is still zero was never written. That one bit of slack pays for two checks: the runtime zeroes the buffer before the launch, and the buffer check afterwards can insist that every entry past n_records is still exactly zero — a probe that strayed outside its slot would show up as a non-zero word where none belongs.

那个 + 1 的作用是让全零的字也能表达一个意思。站点编号从 0 开始，所以没有 + 1 的话，站点 0 在第 0 周期写下的字会和根本没人写过的字无法区分。有了它，每条写过的记录都非零，而仍为零的表项就是从未写过。这一位的余量换来两项检查：运行时在启动前把缓冲区清零，事后缓冲区检查就能坚持 n_records 之后的每个表项仍然精确为零——一个越出自己槽位的探针，会以一个本不该非零的字暴露出来。

A run whose sites record register values widens every record. One 32-bit value makes records 8 bytes with the value at +4; one 64-bit value makes them 16 bytes with the value at +8, so that its store is naturally aligned and the decoder reads the u64 exactly. A timestamp site inside a value run writes only word 0 and leaves the value words untouched, so their contents are meaningless rather than wrong templates.py:659–669.

站点要记录寄存器值的运行会把每条记录加宽。一个 32 位值让记录变成 8 字节，值在 +4；一个 64 位值让记录变成 16 字节，值在 +8，这样它的 store 自然对齐，解码器也能精确读出这个 u64。值运行里的时间戳站点只写 word 0，不碰值字，所以那些字的内容是无意义的，而不是错的 templates.py:659–669。

INIT: the slot from the launch geometryINIT：从启动几何得到槽位

INIT answers one question before any record can be written: where is this warp's slot? It needs two things to answer it, the buffer's address and the warp's number, and neither is in the original kernel. The address comes from an appended kernel parameter. A kernel parameter on these GPUs is not passed in a register: the driver copies the whole parameter block into constant bank 0 before the launch, and the kernel reads a parameter from a fixed byte offset in that bank. The block begins at c[0x0][0x210] on sm_90 and at c[0x0][0x380] on sm_103. The splicer adds an 8-byte pointer after the kernel's own parameters and records where it put it, so INIT loads it from c[0x0][param_base + param_offset] with one ULDC.64 straight into the persistent UR pair templates.py:499–546.

在任何记录写出之前，INIT 要回答一个问题：这个 warp 的槽位在哪里？要回答它需要两样东西——缓冲区地址和这个 warp 的编号——而原来的 kernel 里都没有。地址来自一个追加的 kernel 参数。这些 GPU 上的 kernel 参数不是用寄存器传的：驱动在启动前把整个参数块拷进常量 bank 0，kernel 从这个 bank 里一个固定的字节偏移处读参数。参数块在 sm_90 上从 c[0x0][0x210] 开始，在 sm_103 上从 c[0x0][0x380] 开始。拼接器在 kernel 自己的参数之后追加一个 8 字节指针，并记下放在哪里，于是 INIT 用一条 ULDC.64 从 c[0x0][param_base + param_offset] 直接加载到那对持久 UR 里 templates.py:499–546。

The warp's number is computed, not read: no special register holds a global warp id. That is why the geometry is loaded rather than used as an ALU constant operand: the same body is then correct for any grid and block shape, a partial last warp included templates.py:453–495.

warp 的编号是算出来的，不是读出来的：没有哪个特殊寄存器装着全局 warp 编号。INIT 用 S2R 读 SR_CTAID 和 SR_TID，用 uniform 常量加载从常量 bank 0 读出 blockDim 与 gridDim 的四个字，然后用 23 条指令算出 槽位 = block_linear × ⌈每 block 线程数 / 32⌉ + (tid_linear >> 5)。这就是几何要被加载、而不是当作 ALU 常量操作数的原因：同一个主体因此对任何 grid 和 block 形状都正确，包括最后那个不满的 warp templates.py:453–495。

The last ten instructions turn that number into state. 最后十条指令把这个编号变成状态。R2UR 把槽位号搬进一个 uniform 寄存器；UIMAD.WIDE.U32 把它乘以 slot_bytes 再加上缓冲区基址，让那对持久 UR 指向这个 warp 的槽位；UMOV 把持久计数器清零；CS2R 紧挨着读 %globaltimer 和 SM 时钟；S2R SR_VIRTUALSMID 读 SM 编号，LOP3 把第 31 位——HEADER_VALID 标志——或进去；最后一条 STG.E.128 把这四个字作为头部的前 16 字节写出。INIT 紧接在 ptxas 自己的第 0 条指令、即栈指针加载 LDC R1, c[0x0][stack_ptr] 之后，这是 kernel 准备好的最早时刻，也是还没有任何东西被记录的最晚时刻 templates.py:74–96。

时间戳记录是能称为记录的最便宜的东西，它的七条指令分成三件事。捕获：CS2R.32 R4, SR_CLOCKLO 用 4–6 个周期读 SM 时钟，不占 scoreboard。打包：LEA.LO 一条指令就把这个时钟左移 id_bits 位并或上 site_id + 1，ELECT 选出唯一要做 store 的那个 lane。推进与存储：ULOP3 把持久计数器掩码成环下标，UIMAD.WIDE.U32 把这个下标变成表项地址，一条带谓词的 STG.E 把这个字写到槽位基址之后 32 字节处，UIADD3 给计数器加一 templates.py:736–768。值记录多一条值的 store。主体里没有任何东西读 kernel 的状态，也没有任何一条是分支。

EXIT: the calibrationEXIT：校准

The header's second half is written by an island placed before every EXIT instruction in the kernel. Its five instructions are an ELECT, a CS2R of %globaltimer, a CS2R.32 of the SM clock, a MOV that copies the persistent counter out of its uniform register into a GPR, and one STG.E.128 that writes (gt_end, clk_end, n_records) as 16 bytes at +16. Writing them together is what makes them trustworthy: the record count and the clock anchor it is paired with are captured within a few cycles of each other, so no record can be counted after the anchor that is supposed to bound it. A predicated EXIT the warp passes without taking is harmless, because a later calibration simply overwrites this one with a later pair.

头部的后一半由放在 kernel 中每条 EXIT 指令之前的 island 写出。它的五条指令是：一条 ELECT、一条读 %globaltimer 的 CS2R、一条读 SM 时钟的 CS2R.32、一条把持久计数器从 uniform 寄存器复制进 GPR 的 MOV，以及一条把 (gt_end, clk_end, n_records) 作为 16 字节写到 +16 的 STG.E.128。把它们一起写出正是它们可信的理由：记录数和与之配对的时钟锚点在相差几个周期内被取下，所以不可能有记录被算进来却晚于本该框住它的锚点。warp 经过但没有跳走的带谓词 EXIT 无害，因为后面的校准只会用更晚的一对覆盖这一次。

Five instructions is the comfortable case. The template has six register shapes, from one aligned quad down to five separate single GPRs, and the single-GPR form needs a branch-free fix-up for the case where the global timer carries between its HI and LO reads; last of all it will take one bare even pair. The ladder exists because the last instructions of a small kernel have very few dead registers, and a site that cannot be served is refused rather than served badly templates.py:593–655.

五条指令是宽裕的情形。模板有六种寄存器形状，从一组对齐的四元组一直到五个各自独立的单 GPR，而单 GPR 形式需要一处无分支修正，以应付全局计时器在 HI、LO 两次读之间进位的情况；最后它还能只用一对裸的偶数寄存器。这道阶梯之所以存在，是因为小 kernel 最后几条指令的死寄存器极少；而服务不了的站点会被拒绝，而不是被草率地服务 templates.py:593–655。

Why the persistent state is three uniform registers为什么持久状态是三个 uniform 寄存器

INIT keeps three 32-bit registers live until the warp exits: the even pair holding the slot's address, and the counter. In warp mode they are uniform registers, for two reasons. Uniform state is shared by the whole warp, so when two lane groups diverge and each records an event, each takes the next ring entry; per-lane GPR state would give each group its own counter and let them overwrite each other's records. And uniform registers survive USETMAXREG, the instruction a warp-specialised kernel uses to change its own register budget, so one INIT at kernel entry serves every warp role, where GPR state above a role's new cap would simply be gone templates.py:18–26. Thread mode keeps the state in GPRs and may need a hand-off around each role split; the corpus runs warp mode.

INIT 让三个 32 位寄存器一直活到 warp 退出：装着槽位地址的那对偶数寄存器，以及计数器。warp 模式下它们是 uniform 寄存器，原因有二。uniform 状态由整个 warp 共享，所以当两个 lane 组发生分歧、各自记录一个事件时，它们各占下一个环表项；而每 lane 的 GPR 状态会让每组各有一份计数器，互相覆盖对方的记录。而且 uniform 寄存器能在 USETMAXREG——warp 特化 kernel 用来改变自己寄存器预算的那条指令——之后幸存，所以 kernel 入口的一个 INIT 就能服务每一种 warp 角色，而高于角色新上限的 GPR 状态会直接丢失 templates.py:18–26。thread 模式把状态留在 GPR 里，在每次角色切分前后可能需要一次交接；语料库运行的是 warp 模式。

The ring and the budget ladder环与预算阶梯

The counter counts records and never wraps; a record goes to entry count & (ring_depth − 1). A slot that writes more records than the ring is deep therefore overwrites its own oldest records, and the decoder knows exactly which survived: the oldest surviving entry is n_records mod ring_depth, and min(n_records, ring_depth) records are readable record.py:127–143. Because n_records is in the header, a wrap is never silent. The runtime fails such a run and repeats the whole set at the next power of two, up to --max-ring, keeping every attempt as its own d<depth>/ directory so the discarded one is still on disk. DeepGEMM k512's trace-pp warps write 130 records in a tile loop, so at the default depth of 64, 1,188 slots wrapped and the set was re-run at 256.

计数器数的是记录数，从不回绕；一条记录写到表项 count & (ring_depth − 1)。因此写入条数超过环深度的槽位会覆盖自己最旧的记录，而解码器确切知道哪些幸存下来：最早幸存的表项是 n_records mod ring_depth，可读的记录是 min(n_records, ring_depth) 条 record.py:127–143。因为 n_records 就在头部里，回绕从不无声。运行时判这次运行失败，并以下一个 2 的幂重跑整个集合，直到 --max-ring，每次尝试各自保留为一个 d<depth>/ 目录，所以被丢弃的那次仍在磁盘上。DeepGEMM k512 的 trace-pp warp 在 tile 循环里写 130 条记录，所以在默认深度 64 下有 1,188 个槽位回绕，集合以 256 重跑。

Clocks and calibration时钟与校准

Record format v1 rests on clock facts Olympus measured, not on datasheet ones clockfacts.py:1–26. The SM clock is a counter inside each SM that advances once per clock cycle — 1.98 GHz on H200 and 2.07 GHz on GB300 under load — and CS2R reads its low 32 bits in 4–6 cycles without claiming a scoreboard, which is what makes it affordable in a record. What it is not is a time base. It is not reset when a kernel launches, so its absolute value means nothing on its own. And every SM has its own: two SMs' counters were measured 6 to 12 ms apart on H200 and up to about 1,150 s apart on GB300. Two records from two warps on two SMs are therefore not comparable as cycle numbers at all.

记录格式 v1 建立在 Olympus 自己测出的时钟事实上，而不是数据手册上的 clockfacts.py:1–26。SM 时钟是每个 SM 内部的一个计数器，每个时钟周期走一格——负载下 H200 为 1.98 GHz、GB300 为 2.07 GHz——而 CS2R 用 4–6 个周期读出它的低 32 位且不占用 scoreboard，这正是它在一条记录里负担得起的原因。它不是时间基准。kernel 启动时它不复位，所以它的绝对值本身没有意义。而且每个 SM 各有一个：在 H200 上测得两个 SM 的计数器相差 6 到 12 ms，在 GB300 上最多相差约 1,150 s。因此两个 SM 上两个 warp 的两条记录，作为周期数根本无法比较。

%globaltimer is a time base, shared by the whole GPU, but it advances in 32 ns steps, which is coarser than the gap between consecutive sites in a real kernel. So record format v1 uses both clocks in the one way that needs neither to be perfect: every record carries the cheap SM clock, and INIT and EXIT each read the pair (%globaltimer, SM clock) back to back, giving the slot two anchors in both units. The decoder converts that warp's cycles to nanoseconds from its own two anchors and nothing else.

%globaltimer 是时间基准，由整块 GPU 共享，但它以 32 ns 为步长前进，比真实 kernel 中相邻站点之间的间隔还粗。所以记录格式 v1 用一种对两个时钟都不要求完美的方式同时使用它们：每条记录携带便宜的 SM 时钟，而 INIT 和 EXIT 各自紧挨着读一对（%globaltimer, SM 时钟），给这个槽位两个两种单位都有的锚点。解码器只凭这个 warp 自己的两个锚点，把它的周期换算成纳秒。

# record.py: the decoder's calibration, integer arithmetic throughout (values from the slot used below)
span_clk = clk_end_full - clk_start    # cycles between this slot's two anchors   (52,397)
span_ns  = gt_end - gt_start           # nanoseconds between the same two anchors (30,816)
ns = gt_start + ((clk - clk_start) * span_ns + span_clk // 2) // span_clk

No frequency appears in that formula, and that is the point. A warp that ran while the GPU was boosting and a warp that ran while it was throttled are each converted at the rate their own anchors imply, and whatever offset an SM's counter has from every other SM's cancels, because the same offset is in both of that slot's anchors. The slot below implies 52,397 cycles in 30,816 ns, which is 1.700 GHz — well under the 1.98 GHz the microbenchmarks measure under sustained load, and nothing has to know that for the nanoseconds to come out right.

这个式子里没有出现任何频率，这正是关键。一个在 GPU 升频时运行的 warp 和一个在降频时运行的 warp，各自按自己锚点所蕴含的速率换算；而某个 SM 的计数器相对其他所有 SM 的偏移会被抵消，因为同一个偏移同时存在于这个槽位的两个锚点里。下面那个槽位蕴含的是 30,816 ns 内 52,397 个周期，即 1.700 GHz——远低于微基准在持续负载下测到的 1.98 GHz，而要让纳秒算对，谁都不需要知道这一点。

The arithmetic is integer on purpose. %globaltimer counts from the machine's boot, so gt_start is a number near 1.8 × 1018 ns. A 64-bit float carries 53 bits of mantissa, so at that magnitude the spacing between representable values is 256 ns: adding a floating-point offset to gt_start would round every record to the nearest 256 ns and throw away exactly the resolution the SM clock was carried for. The integer form, with span_clk // 2 added before the division to round instead of truncate, keeps every record exact to half a cycle record.py:144–164. In the standalone proof the calibrated times land within [−28, +43] ns of the kernel's own timer reads on H200 and [−51, +62] ns on GB300 ENV.md:274–287.

这里的算术是有意用整数的。%globaltimer 从机器开机开始计数，所以 gt_start 是一个接近 1.8 × 1018 ns 的数。64 位浮点有 53 位尾数，在这个量级上可表示值之间的间距是 256 ns：往 gt_start 上加一个浮点偏移，会把每条记录舍到最近的 256 ns，恰好扔掉了当初携带 SM 时钟所为的那份分辨率。整数形式——在除法前加上 span_clk // 2 以做四舍五入而非截断——让每条记录精确到半个周期 record.py:144–164。在独立证明中，校准后的时间与 kernel 自己读到的计时器相差：H200 上在 [−28, +43] ns 内，GB300 上在 [−51, +62] ns 内 ENV.md:274–287。

One thing is given up for the shared word: a record keeps only the low 32 − id_bits bits of the clock. At the default 6 that is 26 bits, which wrap every 33.9 ms at H200's 1.98 GHz and every 32.4 ms at GB300's 2.07 GHz. The bound takes the fastest clock the part is seen to run at rather than any one run's calibrated rate, because a faster clock wraps sooner and the bound has to hold for every run. The decoder restores the missing high bits by unwrapping: it walks the ring backwards from clk_end, and at each step takes the one value congruent to the stored 26 bits that is not later than the record after it record.py:110–125. That reconstruction is exact as long as consecutive records of one slot are less than one wrap period apart, which a kernel that finishes in microseconds makes true with four orders of magnitude to spare.

为了共用一个字，有一样东西被放弃了：一条记录只保留时钟低 32 − id_bits 位。默认为 6 时就是 26 位，在 H200 的 1.98 GHz 下每 33.9 ms 回绕一次，在 GB300 的 2.07 GHz 下每 32.4 ms 回绕一次。这个界取的是该型号被观测到的最快时钟，而不是某一次运行校准出的速率：时钟越快回绕越早，而这个界必须对每一次运行都成立。解码器用"展开"把缺掉的高位补回来：它从 clk_end 开始沿环往回走，每一步都取与存下的那 26 位同余、且不晚于其后那条记录的那个值 record.py:110–125。只要同一槽位相邻记录的间隔小于一个回绕周期，这个重建就是精确的；而一个在微秒级结束的 kernel 让这一条以四个数量级的余量成立。

The standalone proof独立证明

Before any splicer or runtime exists for an architecture, the templates are proven alone. A hand-assembled kernel runs INIT, records of every kind, a divergent branch and the EXIT calibration on a 3-D grid of 3-D blocks whose last warp is partial, writing into a poisoned buffer with guard bands on both sides. Every slot is then decoded with decode_slot and checked for header validity, record count, site order, exact values, monotonic unwrapped clocks, nanoseconds against the kernel's own timer reads, and no byte written outside the header and the records; and the same kernel without probes must produce identical output standalone.py:1–22. It passed 11 of 11 configurations on 132 SMs with 0 stray bytes (DECISIONS 40). This is the probes layer's share of invariant 5: nothing runs before it is checked.

在某个架构上还没有任何拼接器或运行时之前，模板先被单独证明。一个手工汇编的 kernel 在一个 3 维 grid、3 维 block、最后一个 warp 不满的配置上运行 INIT、各种记录、一个分歧分支和 EXIT 校准，写进两侧带保护带的投毒缓冲区。随后每个槽位用 decode_slot 解码，核对头部有效、记录数、站点顺序、精确的值、单调的展开时钟、与 kernel 自己读到的计时器相比的纳秒，以及头部与记录之外没有任何字节被写；而同一个 kernel 不带探针时必须给出相同的输出 standalone.py:1–22。它在 132 个 SM 上以 11 个配置全部通过，0 个多余字节（DECISIONS 40）。这是探针层在不变量 5 中承担的那一份：任何东西在被检查之前都不运行。

One record, end to end一条记录，从头到尾

Everything above meets in four bytes. These are real: slot 0 of the DeepGEMM 1d2d k512 kernel's trace-pp run — block 0, warp 0, the math warp — read back from the final sweep's .olyt file, at id_bits = 6, ring depth 256, no values. The record we will follow is the one site 19 wrote on its first tile.

上面的一切都汇合在四个字节里。下面这些是真实的：DeepGEMM 1d2d k512 这个 kernel 的 trace-pp 运行的第 0 号槽位——block 0、warp 0，也就是 math warp——从最终扫描的 .olyt 文件里读回，id_bits = 6，环深度 256，不带值。我们要追的那条记录，是站点 19 在它第一个 tile 上写下的那条。

  1. The site. In this run's sites.yaml, site 19 of the trace-pp set is instruction index 1100, labelled c.tile_done: the end of one tile in the math warp. Its probe runs before instruction 1100, on every path that reaches it.
  2. The body. needs_record asks for one GPR, one even UR pair and one predicate; the planner hands over R4, {UR0,UR1} and P0, with the persistent state already in {UR60,UR61} and UR62. record returns 7 instructions: CS2R.32, LEA.LO, ELECT, ULOP3.LUT, UIMAD.WIDE.U32, STG.E, UIADD3, every one of them at [7:7:{}:15:1].
  3. The island. The search planner of chapter 05 reorders those 7 and chooses their control bits, adding one NOP that waits on the island's own scoreboard — the 8 instructions shown above. The conservative planner's version of the same body is 10. Three constants are visible in the text: 0x14 is 19 + 1, 0x6 is id_bits, 0xff is ring_depth − 1, and the store's +0x20 is the 32-byte header it has to clear.
  4. The slot. Warp 0 of block 0 is linear warp 0, so INIT's arithmetic gives slot 0 and {UR60,UR61} points at buffer + 0 × 1,056. INIT's STG.E.128 wrote the 16 bytes at +0x00: the two anchors and 0x80000080, which is bit 31 set over SM number 128.
  5. The entry. This was the 21st record the warp wrote, so the counter held 20, and 20 & 255 is 20. The entry's address is slot base + 32 + 20 × 4 = slot base + 0x70. The four bytes stored there are 14 55 72 ef, which little-endian is 0xef725514.
  6. The site id. The low 6 bits: 0xef725514 & 63 = 20, minus 1 is site 19 — c.tile_done, as expected. Had the entry read 00 00 00 00, those low bits would have been 0, which is no site at all, and the decoder would have known the warp never got here.
  7. The clock. The top 26 bits: 0xef725514 >> 6 = 62,769,492 cycles, modulo 226. Unwrapping backwards from clk_end picks the congruent value that fits, 59 × 226 + 62,769,492 = 4,022,192,468, which is clk_start + 10,158 cycles.
  8. The nanosecond. This slot's anchors span 52,397 cycles and 30,816 ns. So ns = 1,790,671,159,981,533,856 + (10,158 × 30,816 + 26,198) ÷ 52,397 = gt_start + 5,974 ns, or 1,790,671,159,981,539,830 ns on the GPU's own global clock. This warp reached its first c.tile_done 5.974 µs after its own INIT.

  2. 主体。needs_record 要一个 GPR、一对偶数 UR 和一个谓词；规划器交出 R4、{UR0,UR1} 和 P0，而持久状态已经在 {UR60,UR61} 和 UR62 里。record 返回 7 条指令：CS2R.32、LEA.LO、ELECT、ULOP3.LUT、UIMAD.WIDE.U32、STG.E、UIADD3，每一条都是 [7:7:{}:15:1]。
  3. island。第 05 章的搜索规划器把这 7 条重排并选定控制位，加上一条等待 island 自己 scoreboard 的 NOP——就是上面那 8 条指令。同一个主体由保守规划器处理则是 10 条。文本里能看到三个常量：0x14 是 19 + 1，0x6 是 id_bits，0xff 是 ring_depth − 1，而 store 的 +0x20 是它必须跨过的 32 字节头部。
  4. 槽位。block 0 的 warp 0 就是线性 warp 0，所以 INIT 的算术给出槽位 0，{UR60,UR61} 指向 buffer + 0 × 1,056。INIT 的 STG.E.128 写出了 +0x00 处的 16 字节：两个锚点，以及 0x80000080，即第 31 位置位、叠在 SM 编号 128 之上。
  5. 表项。这是该 warp 写的第 21 条记录，所以计数器当时是 20，而 20 & 255 就是 20。表项地址是 槽位基址 + 32 + 20 × 4 = 槽位基址 + 0x70。存在那里的四个字节是 14 55 72 ef，按小端就是 0xef725514。
  6. 站点编号。低 6 位：0xef725514 & 63 = 20，减 1 得站点 19——正是 c.tile_done。如果这个表项读出来是 00 00 00 00，那低位就是 0，不对应任何站点，解码器便知道这个 warp 从未到过这里。
  7. 时钟。高 26 位：0xef725514 >> 6 = 62,769,492 个周期，模 226。从 clk_end 向后展开，挑出能对上的那个同余值：59 × 226 + 62,769,492 = 4,022,192,468，也就是 clk_start + 10,158 个周期。
  8. 纳秒。这个槽位的两个锚点跨越 52,397 个周期、30,816 ns。于是 ns = 1,790,671,159,981,533,856 + (10,158 × 30,816 + 26,198) ÷ 52,397 = gt_start + 5,974 ns，按 GPU 自己的全局时钟是 1,790,671,159,981,539,830 ns。这个 warp 在自己的 INIT 之后 5.974 µs 到达它的第一个 c.tile_done。

What the reader does not have is a binary: nothing has chosen which registers are safe at index 1100 or what the island's control bits should be, and nothing has put those 8 instruction words into a cubin. 这一层就到这里为止。读到这里，手上有的是一段控制位中性的正确 SASS 主体，以及一种格式：设备内存里的四个字节能解出一个站点和一个纳秒。手上没有的是一个二进制：还没有任何东西判定下标 1100 处哪些寄存器是安全的、这个 island 的控制位该是什么，也还没有任何东西把那 8 个指令字放进 cubin。第 05 章做第一件，第 06 章做第二件。

§4Where it came from从何而来

The paper's probe library is where the shape comes from: an INIT that takes the warp's slot from the launch geometry and keeps three registers live for the rest of the kernel, and RECORD probes that write a timestamp or a value into that slot paper §4.5. Its clock scheme is the one record format v1 implements: the cheap per-SM clock in every record, calibrated per warp against the global timer rather than converted with an assumed frequency paper §A.2.1. Record format v1 fixes the bits of that design (DECISIONS 11): the ring with its monotonic count, the + 1 that makes zero mean empty, and the EXIT island that writes the second anchor and the count as one self-consistent store. The fallback record forms for sites without dead registers are Olympus's own.

形状来自论文的探针库：一个从启动几何得到 warp 槽位、并在 kernel 剩余部分保持三个寄存器活跃的 INIT，以及往槽位里写时间戳或值的 RECORD 探针 论文 §4.5。它的时钟方案正是记录格式 v1 所实现的：每条记录带便宜的 SM 时钟，按 warp 对照全局计时器校准，而不是用假设的频率换算 论文 §A.2.1。记录格式 v1 把这个设计落实到每一位（DECISIONS 11）：带单调计数的环、让零表示空的 + 1，以及把第二个锚点和计数用一条自洽的 store 写出的 EXIT island。为没有死寄存器的站点准备的后备记录形式是 Olympus 自己的。

Where Olympus departs: the clock read costs cycles, not hundreds of nsOlympus 的不同之处：读时钟花的是几个周期，不是几百 nsDECISIONS 40

The paper gives a global-timer read a cost of hundreds of nanoseconds. Olympus measured both clocks on H200: a CS2R of either costs 4–6 cycles, while the scoreboarded S2R and S2UR cost 27 and 37. The record keeps the SM clock anyway, because it is one 32-bit word and because the global timer's 32 ns steps are too coarse for consecutive sites; but every INIT and EXIT reads both with CS2R, which is what makes the per-slot calibration cheap enough to run on every warp DECISIONS.md:102–108.

论文给一次全局计时器读取标的代价是几百纳秒。Olympus 在 H200 上测了两个时钟：任一时钟的 CS2R 花 4–6 个周期，而经 scoreboard 的 S2R、S2UR 花 27 和 37 个周期。记录仍然保留 SM 时钟，因为它只占一个 32 位字，也因为全局计时器 32 ns 的步长对相邻站点太粗；但每个 INIT 和 EXIT 都用 CS2R 把两者都读一遍，这正是让每槽位校准便宜到能在每个 warp 上运行的原因 DECISIONS.md:102–108。

Where Olympus departs: a fence that assumes no latency model, and the bug it hadOlympus 的不同之处：不依赖任何延迟模型的围栏，以及它曾有的 bugDECISIONS 65

The paper schedules probes with its cost model from the start. Olympus's first planner instead wraps each bare body in a conservative fence: two leading NOPs, every instruction at stall 15, every variable-latency write and every store's source read on a scoreboard the next instruction waits for, and a trailing wait on all six, so the body is correct whatever the latency tables say templates.py:270–286. The fence had one bug, found by fuzzing, not by the sweep: its wait-on-everything sat on the first NOP, so after a kernel instruction that claims a scoreboard with stall 1 the wait issued 1 cycle after the claim and missed it, and a VALUE probe read an in-flight destination (t0_smem_sync index 9 after LDG.E.CONSTANT R2, about 64,000 of 65,536 twin pairs wrong while the outputs stayed bit-exact). The wait now sits on the last lead NOP, at least 15 cycles after the kernel's previous instruction, the 2-cycle rule of invariant 4 applied to the fence itself DECISIONS.md:248–250.

论文从一开始就用它的代价模型调度探针。Olympus 的第一个规划器则把每个裸主体裹进一道保守围栏：开头两条 NOP，每条指令 stall 15，每个可变延迟写和每条 store 的源读都占一个 scoreboard 并由下一条指令等待，结尾等待全部六个，所以无论延迟表怎么说，主体都是正确的 templates.py:270–286。这道围栏有过一个 bug，由模糊测试而非扫描发现：它的全等待放在第一条 NOP 上，于是在一条以 stall 1 占用 scoreboard 的 kernel 指令之后，等待在占用后 1 个周期发射而错过了它，VALUE 探针读到了尚在途中的目的寄存器（t0_smem_sync 第 9 条、LDG.E.CONSTANT R2 之后，65,536 对孪生记录中约 64,000 对出错，而输出保持逐位一致）。现在等待放在最后一条前导 NOP 上，离 kernel 前一条指令至少 15 个周期，即把不变量 4 的 2 周期规则用在围栏自身 DECISIONS.md:248–250。

Where Olympus departs: psave, and the write pointer that stayed a counterOlympus 的不同之处：psave，以及仍是计数器的写指针DECISIONS 71

Two template changes were tried against the overhead target. psave, an elect on a borrowed live predicate instead of the 32-lane noelect store, was mixed everywhere (matmul trace-mem −1.32 points, FlashMLA +0.52), because overhead follows the number of inserted instructions rather than the cost model's 515 vs 30 cycles; it is on by default only where its record is shorter, for 64-bit GPR values, which moved matmul trace-mem from +21.44% to +20.18% with every gate re-passed. A persistent write pointer in place of the counter was dropped: on the islands both plans keep it changed 0.0%, since the ring arithmetic runs beside the clock → store chain, not on it, and a correct form would have needed a read claim or a copy and a format v2 DECISIONS.md:328–341.

针对开销目标试过两处模板改动。psave，即在借来的活跃谓词上做 elect，代替 32 lane 的 noelect store，在所有地方启用时好坏参半（matmul trace-mem −1.32 个百分点，FlashMLA +0.52），因为开销跟随插入指令的数量，而不是代价模型里的 515 对 30 个周期；它只在记录更短的地方默认开启，即 64 位 GPR 值，这把 matmul trace-mem 从 +21.44% 降到 +20.18%，所有的门重新通过。用持久写指针代替计数器的方案被放弃：在两种计划都保留的 island 上它的改变是 0.0%，因为环运算与时钟 → store 链并行，不在链上；而正确的形式需要一次读占用或一次复制，以及格式 v2 DECISIONS.md:328–341。
