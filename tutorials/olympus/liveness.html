Dead registers死寄存器

A probe spliced into compiled SASS brings no registers of its own. It borrows registers the kernel will write again before it reads them. This chapter shows how Olympus finds those registers: backward liveness over the control-flow graph, then everything that makes a register that looks unused unsafe to touch, from in-flight loads and HGMMA groups to warp-role caps. It ends with the check that confirmed the result on all 20 corpus kernels.

拼接进已编译 SASS 的探针自己不带寄存器，它借用的是 kernel 在读取之前会重新写入的寄存器。本章讲 Olympus 怎样找出这些寄存器：先在控制流图上做反向活跃性分析，再排除所有看似没用、实际却碰不得的寄存器，从在途的 load 和 HGMMA 组一直到 warp 角色的寄存器上限。最后介绍在语料库全部 20 个 kernel 上确认了结果的那项检查。

§1What “dead” means“死”是什么意思

A probe spliced into a compiled kernel brings instructions but no registers. Paper §2.3 names this the first challenge, C1: the probe may overwrite a register that still holds a live value, and nothing in the binary says which registers are safe, because the compiler's allocation is gone paper §2.3. Xtrace answers it from the binary itself. A backward dataflow analysis gives each register its live periods, from a write to the value's last read, and a probe may borrow the register in the gaps between them, its dead periods paper §4.3.

拼接进已编译 kernel 的探针只带来指令，不带寄存器。论文 §2.3 把这列为第一个挑战 C1：探针可能改写一个仍装着活跃值的寄存器，而编译器的分配信息已经不在了，二进制里没有任何地方说明哪些寄存器可以安全使用 论文 §2.3。Xtrace 直接从二进制出发解决它：用反向数据流分析求出每个寄存器的活跃区间，即从一次写入到这个值最后一次被读取之间的范围；区间之间的空档就是死区间，探针可以在那里借用这个寄存器 论文 §4.3。

Olympus asks the question once per instruction: which registers are dead before instruction i? An island spliced before i runs on every path that reaches i, branches into i included, so a register counts as dead only if, on every such path, no later read can see a write made there api.py:6–18. The liveness itself is the textbook backward equation over the CFG, live_in(i) = use(i) ∪ (live_out(i) ∖ kill(i)), iterated in reverse post-order until nothing changes analysis.py:170–205. Two SASS details matter. A guarded write such as @P0 MOV may not happen, so it does not kill the old value. A callee's return edges go to every return site analysis.py:1–35. One bitset holds all four register files a probe can use: GPRs R0–R254, uniform registers UR0–UR62, predicates P0–P6 and uniform predicates UP0–UP6 effects.py:12–34.

Olympus 对每条指令提出同一个问题：在指令 i 之前，哪些寄存器是死的？拼接在 i 之前的 island 会在每条到达 i 的路径上执行，跳转到 i 的分支也算在内，所以只有当每条这样的路径上都没有后续读取能看到在这里写下的值时，这个寄存器才算死 api.py:6–18。活跃性本身就是教科书上 CFG 的反向方程 live_in(i) = use(i) ∪ (live_out(i) ∖ kill(i))，按逆后序反复迭代，直到不再变化 analysis.py:170–205。有两处 SASS 细节要注意：@P0 MOV 这样带谓词的写不一定发生，所以不会杀死旧值；被调函数的返回边连到每一个返回点 analysis.py:1–35。探针能用的四种寄存器共用一个位集：GPR R0–R254、uniform 寄存器 UR0–UR62、谓词 P0–P6 和 uniform 谓词 UP0–UP6 effects.py:12–34。

Why is a dead register free? If every path writes the register again before reading it, the value a probe leaves there is overwritten before the kernel can see it. The kernel computes exactly what it computed before, and none of its own instructions changes; the allocation stays as ptxas left it, REGCOUNT included. A probe needs only a few such registers. Olympus's INIT keeps three live for the whole kernel, URs when it records per warp DECISIONS.md:100–101. The paper records the per-SM clock rather than the global timer partly because a 32-bit capture of it fits in one register, where a safe capture of the global timer takes two paper §A.2.1.

为什么死寄存器可以随便用？如果每条路径都会先重新写这个寄存器再读它，探针留在里面的值就会在 kernel 看到之前被覆盖。kernel 算出的结果和原来完全一样，它自己的指令一条都不变；寄存器分配保持 ptxas 生成时的样子，REGCOUNT 也不变。探针需要的这种寄存器不多：Olympus 的 INIT 在整个 kernel 期间只保留 3 个活跃寄存器，按 warp 记录时用的是 UR DECISIONS.md:100–101。论文选择记录每个 SM 的时钟而不是全局计时器，理由之一就是前者的 32 位读数放得进一个寄存器，而安全地读取全局计时器需要两个 论文 §A.2.1。

The rest of this chapter is about the other terms of one mask. A register is dead before i when it lies inside the allocation in force at i and in none of four sets: live, held by an operation still in flight, inside a wgmma fence window, or kept by divergence.

本章其余部分讲的是同一个掩码里的其他几项。寄存器在 i 之前是死的，条件是它位于 i 处生效的分配范围之内，并且不属于以下四个集合中的任何一个：活跃、被仍在途的操作占用、处在 wgmma fence 窗口内、因分叉而保留。

    §2Registers that look free but aren't看似空闲、其实被占用的寄存器

    Backward liveness sees only the instruction stream. Hopper keeps work in flight after an instruction issues, and the hardware interlocks neither the register file nor the pipes, so every hazard is the compiler's to resolve in the control bits paper §A.2.1. A register that an operation still owns can therefore look dead, and a probe that writes it races that operation. Paper §4.3 marks such registers live until the operation completes paper §4.3. Olympus does it with a forward pass, the holds: the operands an issued instruction still owns when control reaches i, joined over paths and followed across back edges analysis.py:208–233 holds.py:1–19. There are three kinds effects.py:12–34:

    反向活跃性分析只看得到指令流。而 Hopper 上的指令发射之后，操作仍在途；硬件既不对寄存器堆加互锁，也不对流水线加互锁，所有冒险都由编译器用控制位来解决 论文 §A.2.1。于是，一个仍被某个操作占着的寄存器看起来可能是死的，探针写它就会与那个操作竞争。论文 §4.3 在操作完成之前把这样的寄存器标为活跃 论文 §4.3。Olympus 用一个前向分析来做这件事，称为占用（hold）：控制流到达 i 时，已发射的指令仍然占有的操作数；它在路径汇合处取并集，并沿回边传递 analysis.py:208–233 holds.py:1–19。占用分三类 effects.py:12–34：

    • In-flight memory and variable-latency operands. A load or store may read its sources after it issues. They stay held until its read scoreboard is waited on or, when it has none, until the kernel itself next writes the register, because ptxas must have resolved that write-after-read by then. A load's destination stays held until its write scoreboard is waited on or, with none, until the kernel next touches it. In axpy, the address R20–R21 of the LDG at 25 is held until the FFMA at 30 waits on SB2.
    • GMMA groups. Every GPR an HGMMA reads or writes is held from issue until the WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, N that retires its group: the accumulators, and the A fragment when A comes from registers. A GMMA that carries gsb0 commits the open group, DEPBAR.LE gsb0, N retires every committed group but the N newest, and WARPGROUP.ARRIVE and WARPGROUP.WAIT release nothing.
    • wgmma fence windows, explained after the next paragraph.
    • 在途的访存与变延迟操作数。load 或 store 可能在发射之后才读取源操作数。这些源寄存器一直被占用，直到它的读 scoreboard 被等待；如果它没有读 scoreboard，就占用到 kernel 自己下一次写这个寄存器为止，因为到那时 ptxas 必定已经解决了这个读后写冒险。load 的目的寄存器一直被占用，直到它的写 scoreboard 被等待；没有写 scoreboard 时，占用到 kernel 下一次访问它为止。在 axpy 中，25 处 LDG 的地址 R20–R21 一直被占用，直到 30 处的 FFMA 等待 SB2。
    • GMMA 组。HGMMA 读写的每个 GPR 都从发射起被占用，直到让它所在组退休的 WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, N：包括累加器，以及 A 来自寄存器时的 A 片段。带 gsb0 的 GMMA 提交当前打开的组，DEPBAR.LE gsb0, N 让除最新 N 个以外的所有已提交组退休，WARPGROUP.ARRIVE 和 WARPGROUP.WAIT 什么也不释放。
    • wgmma fence 窗口，在下一段之后讲。

    The register-A case is the one that failed in practice. t1_attention's P·V product reads its A fragment from R152–R183. After the last HGMMA, at 773, no instruction reads those registers again, so liveness alone frees them from 774 on. But the group is still in flight, across the loop's back edge at 792, until the WARPGROUP.DEPBAR at 326 in the next iteration. G-Watch 0.0.35's liveness treats register A operands as dead once issued, so its probes wrote them, in 24 probe blocks of trace-pp and 44 of trace-mem's first chunk, and the traced o was wrong. It never touched the accumulators DECISIONS.md:57.

    真正出过问题的是寄存器 A 操作数。t1_attention 的 P·V 乘积从 R152–R183 读取 A 片段。773 处最后一条 HGMMA 之后再没有指令读这些寄存器，所以只看活跃性的话，它们从 774 起就空闲了。但这个组仍然在途，跨过 792 处的循环回边，一直持续到下一轮迭代 326 处的 WARPGROUP.DEPBAR。G-Watch 0.0.35 的活跃性分析在发射之后就把寄存器 A 操作数当作死寄存器，它的探针于是写了这些寄存器：trace-pp 中有 24 个探针块、trace-mem 第一块中有 44 个，追踪得到的 o 是错的。累加器它从来没有碰过 DECISIONS.md:57。

    The third exclusion was found by the check in §4 rather than by reading PTX first. At t1_attention 293, WARPGROUP.ARRIVE, which is wgmma.fence, comes before eight HGMMAs. The ones at 298 and 316 start their accumulators, R56–R87 and R24–R55, from zero (RZ, !UPT), so they only write them. Liveness calls those registers dead between the fence and the HGMMA, since their old values are never read. But PTX requires a fence between any register access and a later wgmma that accesses the same registers, and calls anything else undefined. Islands after the ARRIVE that wrote those accumulators changed the kernel's m and o in 8 of 9 runs, while the same islands before it matched in 3 of 3 DECISIONS.md:198. The fix is a backward pass: fence_in(i) is empty at a fence and otherwise holds the GPRs of every GMMA reachable from i without passing one analysis.py:322–360. At 294 it takes 41 GPRs out of the free set, leaving 10 of 51. The ARRIVE's own island runs before the fence, so it keeps all 51.

    第三种排除情形是 §4 的检查发现的，而不是先读 PTX 读出来的。t1_attention 的 293 处是 WARPGROUP.ARRIVE（即 wgmma.fence），后面跟着八条 HGMMA。其中 298 和 316 处的两条让累加器 R56–R87 与 R24–R55 从零开始（RZ, !UPT），所以只写不读。活跃性分析认为这些寄存器在 fence 与 HGMMA 之间是死的，因为它们的旧值不会被读取。但 PTX 要求：对寄存器的任何访问，与之后访问同一批寄存器的 wgmma 之间，必须隔着 fence，否则行为未定义。在 ARRIVE 之后写这些累加器的 island，9 次运行中有 8 次改变了 kernel 的 m 和 o；同样的 island 放在 ARRIVE 之前，3 次都一致 DECISIONS.md:198。修复是一个反向分析：fence_in(i) 在 fence 处为空，其余位置包含从 i 出发、不经过 fence 就能到达的每条 GMMA 的 GPR analysis.py:322–360。在 294 处，它从空闲集合中拿走 41 个 GPR，51 个只剩 10 个。ARRIVE 自己的 island 在 fence 之前执行，所以仍有 51 个可用。

    §3Hardware facts硬件事实

    The top two GPRs are reserved顶端的两个 GPR 是保留的

    REGCOUNT counts two registers that no code names. Wherever a corpus kernel reaches the top of its allocation, its highest register is exactly REGCOUNT − 3, and an island that writes either of the top two faults with “illegal instruction”. The dead-register check measured it. With REGCOUNT 16, R14 and R15 fault and R12 and R13 do not; with REGCOUNT 255, R253 faults and R252 does not; under DeepGEMM's USETMAXREG caps, R38 faults at 40 and R230 at 232 analysis.py:50–63 DECISIONS.md:126–130. So a probe may use R0 to R(cap − 3) of whatever cap is in force. In the heatmap these are the hatched rows: R24–R25 in axpy and R188–R189 in attention.

    REGCOUNT 里算进了两个任何代码都不会用到的寄存器。只要语料库中某个 kernel 用到了分配的顶端，它用到的最高寄存器恰好是 REGCOUNT − 3；写顶端两个寄存器中任何一个的 island 都会以 “illegal instruction” 出错。这是死寄存器检查测出来的：REGCOUNT 为 16 时，R14 和 R15 出错，R12 和 R13 不会；REGCOUNT 为 255 时，R253 出错，R252 不会；在 DeepGEMM 的 USETMAXREG 上限下，上限 40 时 R38 出错，上限 232 时 R230 出错 analysis.py:50–63 DECISIONS.md:126–130。所以探针只能用当前生效上限下的 R0 到 R(上限 − 3)。在热力图里，它们就是带斜线的行：axpy 中的 R24–R25，attention 中的 R188–R189。

    Warp roles and USETMAXREGwarp 角色与 USETMAXREG

    Warp-specialized kernels move registers between roles at run time. Typically the producer warps that issue TMA loads give registers back with USETMAXREG.DEALLOC, and the consumer warps that run the HGMMAs take more with USETMAXREG.TRY_ALLOC. Paper §4.3 derives each role's cap from the budget encoded in that instruction paper §4.3. Olympus computes the cap in force at every point with a forward pass that takes the minimum over paths. The cap is REGCOUNT at the entry and n after DEALLOC n. After TRY_ALLOC n it is min(old, n) until the success edge of the instruction's retry loop, where it becomes n analysis.py:236–305 DECISIONS.md:74–80. GPRs above the cap are outside the allocation. The heatmap's DeepGEMM window shows all three of that kernel's caps within 30 instructions.

    warp 专用化（warp-specialized）的 kernel 会在运行时在角色之间转移寄存器：通常，发出 TMA 加载的生产者 warp 用 USETMAXREG.DEALLOC 交还寄存器，执行 HGMMA 的消费者 warp 用 USETMAXREG.TRY_ALLOC 申请更多。论文 §4.3 从这条指令里编码的预算推出每个角色的上限 论文 §4.3。Olympus 用一个在路径汇合处取最小值的前向分析，算出每个位置生效的上限：入口处是 REGCOUNT，DEALLOC n 之后是 n；TRY_ALLOC n 之后先取 min(旧值, n)，到这条指令的重试循环的成功边上才变为 n analysis.py:236–305 DECISIONS.md:74–80。高于上限的 GPR 在分配之外。热力图里的 DeepGEMM 窗口在 30 条指令之内就出现了这个 kernel 的全部三个上限。

    Uniform registers under divergence分叉下的 uniform 寄存器

    A GPR is private to each lane, but a UR or UP is one register for the whole warp. While a warp is split into lane groups that run independently, under the independent thread scheduling Volta introduced, an island that one group runs overwrites the uniform registers of every other group too. So a UR is dead before p only if it is dead at p and at every point another group may be at meanwhile divergence.py:1–116. Olympus models where the other groups may be. A branch that can split its group opens a region. The region ends at an instruction measured on H200 to reconverge a split warp: the BSYNC of a convergence region, or a WARPSYNC.ALL. BAR.SYNC and WARPGROUP.* do not end one. A branch that cannot split its group opens no region: a .U or .DIV branch, or one whose condition every lane of the group shares, such as a uniform register, a SHFL broadcast or an mbarrier phase check. In an ELECT region, the elected lane only has to spare the other lanes' registers.

    GPR 每个 lane 各有一份，而 UR 或 UP 是整个 warp 共用的一个寄存器。在 Volta 引入的独立线程调度下，warp 可能分裂成若干独立执行的 lane 组，这时其中一组执行的 island 会连其他组的 uniform 寄存器一起改写。所以 UR 在 p 之前是死的，前提是它在 p 处是死的，并且在这期间其他组可能所在的每个位置上也都是死的 divergence.py:1–116。Olympus 对其他组可能在哪里建模：能把组拆开的分支开启一个区域，区域结束于在 H200 上实测能让分裂的 warp 重新汇合的指令，即收敛区域的 BSYNC，或 WARPSYNC.ALL；BAR.SYNC 和 WARPGROUP.* 都不能结束区域。不会拆开组的分支不开启区域：.U 或 .DIV 分支，以及条件取决于组内所有 lane 共有的值的分支，比如 uniform 寄存器、SHFL 广播或 mbarrier 相位检查。在 ELECT 区域里，被选中的 lane 只需避开其他 lane 的寄存器。

    The check shaped these rules too. In the CUTLASS GEMM (t2), the elected TMA producer lane polls with TRYWAIT and NANOSLEEP.SYNCS while the other 31 lanes pass two BSYNC instructions, because a BSYNC releases its waiting lanes early when a participant sleeps or yields. An island at 1660 or 1817 that wrote the descriptor pointer UR28 faulted with a misaligned address, and such a BSYNC no longer counts as a reconvergence point. The rule only ever removes uniform registers from the dead set. It matters where roles branch per lane: FlashMLA has 140 divergent branches, the CUTLASS GEMM 72 and FA-3 64, while t1_attention has none. None of the heatmap's four windows has a cell that divergence alone excludes.

    这些规则同样是被检查逼出来的。在 CUTLASS GEMM（t2）中，被选中的 TMA 生产者 lane 用 TRYWAIT 和 NANOSLEEP.SYNCS 轮询，而另外 31 个 lane 越过了两个 BSYNC，因为当某个参与者睡眠或让出时，BSYNC 会提前放行正在等待的 lane。于是在 1660 或 1817 处写描述符指针 UR28 的 island 以未对齐地址出错；这样的 BSYNC 从此不再算作重新汇合点。这条规则只会把 uniform 寄存器从死寄存器集合中移出。它在按 lane 划分角色的 kernel 里影响最大：FlashMLA 有 140 条分叉分支，CUTLASS GEMM 有 72 条，FA-3 有 64 条，而 t1_attention 一条也没有。热力图的四个窗口里，没有哪个格子是只因分叉而被排除的。

    §4How the analysis was validated分析是怎样验证的

    An analysis that is wrong about one register at one point corrupts a kernel silently, so Olympus tests its claim directly, with the dead-register check that was M3's exit deadcheck.py:1–54. At each test point, which is every reachable instruction of a corpus kernel, it splices an island that sets every register dead_before(i) reports to a known test value: GPRs, URs, predicates and uniform predicates. Then it runs the kernel on its usual inputs and compares every output byte with the stored reference. If the analysis is right, the kernel writes each of those registers again before reading it, so the outputs cannot change.

    分析只要在一个位置上把一个寄存器判错，就会悄无声息地破坏 kernel，所以 Olympus 直接检验自己的结论，这就是作为 M3 完成标准的死寄存器检查 deadcheck.py:1–54。在每个测试点（即语料库 kernel 的每条可达指令）上，它拼入一个 island，把 dead_before(i) 报告的每个寄存器都设为已知的测试值：GPR、UR、谓词和 uniform 谓词都包括在内。然后用平常的输入运行 kernel，把每个输出字节与保存的参考结果比较。如果分析正确，kernel 会在读取这些寄存器之前重新写入它们，输出就不可能改变。

    The check is built so that a pass means something. It runs in two phases with complementary values, 0x5a5a5a5a with true predicates and then 0xa5a5a5a5 with false ones, so every bit of every overwritten register takes both values. The islands use immediates only, wait on no scoreboard and claim none. Before anything runs, every island word is checked three ways: by Olympus's decoder, by the offline splice proof and against cuobjdump's disassembly. A batch whose outputs differ, or that faults or hangs, is bisected to a single point and then to a minimal set of registers, which becomes the reproducer.

    这项检查的设计保证了“通过”是有意义的。它分两个阶段写入互补的值：先写 0x5a5a5a5a 并把谓词置真，再写 0xa5a5a5a5 并把谓词置假，这样每个被改写寄存器的每一位都取过两种值。island 只用立即数，不等待任何 scoreboard，也不占用任何 scoreboard。在运行任何东西之前，每个 island 指令字都经过三重核对：Olympus 的解码器、离线拼接证明，以及 cuobjdump 的反汇编。输出不一致、出错或挂起的批次会被二分到单个测试点，再二分到最小的寄存器集合，这就是复现用例。

    A check that never fails proves nothing, so every case also runs controls: runs that overwrite registers the analysis says are live, such as a store's data GPRs, its guard predicate or its address UR. These must change the outputs. The GPR control changed them in all 20 cases, which shows that the islands run on that launch path and that a wrong analysis would be caught EVAL.md:26.

    从不失败的检查什么也证明不了，所以每个用例还要跑对照：改写分析认为活跃的寄存器，例如 store 的数据 GPR、它的 guard 谓词或地址 UR。这些改写必须改变输出。GPR 对照在全部 20 个用例中都改变了输出，这说明 island 确实在这条启动路径上执行，分析一旦出错就会被发现 EVAL.md:26。

    §5Why it matters意义何在

    Paper §5.5 asks whether dead-period registers are enough for the probes. On its three NVIDIA kernels they fed every splice without a fallback and without adding a register, even at the insertion points of peak register pressure, where in-flight Tensor Core operations hold accumulators; the tightest still kept 6 to 9 registers free paper §5.5. Olympus's corpus has tighter spots than that. The strip shows, for each kernel, how many GPRs are dead at each point under the cap in force, and marks below it the trace sites that only Olympus traces.

    论文 §5.5 考察死区间寄存器是否够探针使用。在它的三个 NVIDIA kernel 上，每一次拼接都只靠死区间寄存器完成，既没有回退，也没有增加寄存器；即使在寄存器压力最大、在途 Tensor Core 操作占着累加器的插入点，最紧的地方也还有 6 到 9 个空闲寄存器 论文 §5.5。Olympus 的语料库里有比这更紧的地方。下面的条带图对每个 kernel 画出在当前生效上限下每个位置有多少死 GPR，并在下方标出只有 Olympus 能追踪的站点。
