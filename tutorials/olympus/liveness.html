Knowing where it's safe知道哪里安全

A probe spliced into a compiled kernel brings instructions but no registers; the compiler's allocation is gone, and nothing in the binary says which registers are free. The liveness layer answers that question once per instruction, from the binary alone: which registers may an island placed before instruction i overwrite? The answer counts not only the registers the kernel will read next but the ones an operation still in flight owns, and it is proven, not assumed: a check overwrites every register the analysis calls dead and requires the kernel's outputs to stay bit-identical.

拼接进已编译 kernel 的探针只带来指令，不带寄存器；编译器的分配信息已经不在，二进制里没有任何地方说明哪些寄存器是空闲的。活跃性层只凭二进制、对每条指令回答一次这个问题：放在指令 i 之前的 island 可以改写哪些寄存器？答案不仅算上 kernel 接下来会读的寄存器，也算上仍在途的操作所占有的那些；而且它是被证明的，不是被假设的：一项检查改写分析判为已死的每个寄存器，并要求 kernel 的输出逐位不变。

§1Contract契约

What this layer promises这一层的承诺

A register the analysis calls dead at a point can be overwritten there without changing the kernel's outputs.

分析在某个位置判为已死的寄存器，可以在那里被改写，而不改变 kernel 的输出。

olympus/cfg → olympus/liveness → used by使用者 olympus/hazards · olympus/planner · olympus/verify

The promise is the one line of ARCHITECTURE.md's table for this layer ARCHITECTURE.md:46. It rests on the layer below it in the same chapter, the control-flow graph: the blocks, edges, dominators and loops that the analyses and the splicer both use ARCHITECTURE.md:43. Every register a probe writes comes from this layer's answer, and three layers ahead of it ask for that answer in three different shapes. The hazard model, which sets how far apart a probe's instructions and the kernel's have to be (chapter 03), asks free_scoreboards(i): which of the six per-warp scoreboards no operation still in flight is using. The search planner, which decides which register each probe instruction writes (chapter 05), asks dead_before(i) at every site and partitions the answer among the probe's registers. The dead-register check asks both questions and then spends GPU time trying to falsify the answers api.py:53–67.

这个承诺就是 ARCHITECTURE.md 表格里这一层的那一行 ARCHITECTURE.md:46。它建立在本章下半部分、控制流图之上：分析与拼接器共用的基本块、边、支配关系和循环 ARCHITECTURE.md:43。探针写的每一个寄存器都来自这一层的答案，而它上面的三层以三种不同形式来要这个答案。冒险模型决定探针的指令与 kernel 的指令之间必须隔多远（第 03 章），它问 free_scoreboards(i)：每个 warp 的六个 scoreboard 里，哪些没有被仍在途的操作占着。搜索规划器决定探针的每条指令写哪个寄存器（第 05 章），它在每个站点问 dead_before(i)，再把答案分配给探针的各个寄存器。死寄存器检查把这两个问题都问一遍，然后花 GPU 时间设法证伪答案 api.py:53–67。

Without that answer there is no probe at all, and the reason is the shape of the failure. A register written while the kernel still needs its value does not fault. The kernel computes something else and reports nothing, and the probe's own records look perfectly well formed. So the contract is phrased about the kernel's outputs rather than about registers, because the outputs are the thing that can be checked byte for byte. It is also why no layer above this one reasons about liveness on its own: a site where too few registers are dead is refused by the planner, or handed to an explicit fallback (the per-warp downgrade, or an opt-in register raise), never patched over by a guess.

没有这个答案就根本没有探针，而原因在于失败的形态。一个在 kernel 仍需要其值时被改写的寄存器不会出错。kernel 只是算出别的东西，什么也不说，而探针自己的记录看起来完全正常。所以契约是关于 kernel 输出来表述的，而不是关于寄存器，因为输出才是可以逐字节检查的东西。这也是为什么这一层之上没有哪一层自行推断活跃性：死寄存器太少的站点由规划器拒绝，或交给一条明确的回退路径（降级为按 warp 记录，或经选择启用的寄存器抬升），绝不靠猜测糊弄过去。

§2Invariant不变量

Invariant不变量2

A probe uses only registers proven dead, including registers held by in-flight asynchronous operations until their release point.

探针只使用被证明已死的寄存器，包括被在途异步操作占用、直到释放点才空出的寄存器。

Checked by the dead-register check, verify/deadcheck.py, which overwrites every register the analysis calls dead at every reachable instruction and requires bit-identical outputs ARCHITECTURE.md:61–63: 20 / 20 corpus kernels on H200, 10 / 10 T0 kernels and both DeepGEMM sm100 kernels on GB300, with controls that overwrite a live register and must change the outputs (EVAL.md).

检查方式：死寄存器检查 verify/deadcheck.py，在每条可达指令处改写分析判为已死的每个寄存器，并要求输出逐位一致 ARCHITECTURE.md:61–63：H200 上 20 / 20 个语料库 kernel，GB300 上 10 / 10 个 T0 kernel 和两个 DeepGEMM sm100 kernel，另有改写活跃寄存器、必须改变输出的对照（EVAL.md）。

The weaker invariant would be the textbook one: a probe uses only registers that are not live. On this hardware that is not enough. A Hopper SM does not interlock its register file or its pipelines, which is to say the hardware will not hold an instruction back because an earlier one has not finished with a register; chapter 01 is where that appears, as in-order issue with no interlocks. ptxas is what makes a kernel safe in spite of it. When it schedules the kernel it either leaves enough cycles between two instructions or sets a scoreboard on the first and a wait bit on the second. The consequence for us is that program order is not the whole story. A load issued five instructions ago is still in flight: the hardware will write its destination register later, and it may not have read its address registers yet. Backward liveness sees neither event, because neither is a read or a write in the instruction stream after the load. A probe that takes such a register is racing the hardware, and it is a race, not a certainty: when the analysis still missed the wgmma fence window, 3 of 3 runs matched before the fence and 8 of 9 differed after it (DECISIONS 55). So the invariant says proven dead, with the holds inside the definition of dead, and the check runs the kernel rather than inspecting the analysis.

更弱的不变量会是教科书上那个：探针只使用不活跃的寄存器。在这种硬件上它不够。Hopper 的 SM 既不对寄存器文件也不对流水线做互锁，也就是说，硬件不会因为前一条指令还没用完某个寄存器就压住后一条指令；这一点在第 01 章出现过，表现为按序发射、没有互锁。让 kernel 仍然安全的是 ptxas。它在调度 kernel 时，要么在两条指令之间留出足够的周期，要么在前一条上设置 scoreboard、在后一条上设置等待位。这对我们的后果是：程序顺序并不是全部。五条指令之前发射的一次 load 仍然在途：硬件稍后才会写它的目的寄存器，而它也可能还没有读自己的地址寄存器。反向活跃性看不到这两件事，因为在这次 load 之后的指令流里，两者都不是一次读或一次写。拿走这种寄存器的探针是在与硬件赛跑，而这是赛跑，不是必然：当分析还没有算上 wgmma 的 fence 窗口时，fence 之前 3 次运行 3 次一致，fence 之后 9 次里 8 次不同（DECISIONS 55）。所以不变量说的是被证明已死，把占用写进"死"的定义本身，而检查是真的运行 kernel，不是审视分析。

§3How it works如何工作

What live and dead have to mean活跃与已死必须是什么意思

Start with the two words. A register is live at a point in the program if some later instruction can read it before anything writes it again. It is dead if no such read exists. "Later" means along the kernel's control flow, not down the listing: the question is whether any path forward from this point reads the value that is in the register now. Liveness is therefore a property of a point and a register together, never of a register alone. R20 can be live before one instruction and dead before the next.

先说清这两个词。如果在程序的某个位置之后，还有指令可能在它被重写之前读到它，这个寄存器在该位置就是活跃的。如果不存在这样的读取，它就是已死的。"之后"指的是沿 kernel 的控制流，不是顺着清单往下：问题在于，从这个位置往前走的任何一条路径，是否会读到寄存器里现在这个值。所以活跃性是"位置 + 寄存器"共同的属性，绝不是寄存器单独的属性。R20 可以在一条指令之前活跃，而在下一条指令之前已死。

That phrasing is why the analysis runs backward. Operationally the question at a point is "is this value ever read again on any path from here?", and the only way to answer it is to look at what comes after. So the analysis starts at the kernel's exits, where nothing is live, and walks the instructions in reverse. An instruction that reads a register makes it live at the point before itself. An instruction that writes a register, with no guard predicate on it, makes it dead at the point before itself, because whatever was there can no longer be observed. Where control flow joins, which happens whenever two instructions can branch to the same place, the answer is the union of the successors' answers. A register is dead only when every path forward ignores it, which is why a register dead on one path is not dead at a point both paths reach.

正是这种表述决定了分析要反向进行。具体地说，某个位置上的问题是"从这里出发的任何路径上，这个值还会被读到吗？"，而回答它的唯一办法是看后面有什么。所以分析从 kernel 的各个出口出发——那里什么都不活跃——然后逆着指令往回走。读某个寄存器的指令，使它在自己之前的那个位置变为活跃。写某个寄存器、且没有 guard 谓词的指令，使它在自己之前的那个位置变为已死，因为原来的内容再也无法被观察到。控制流汇合的地方——只要两条指令可以跳到同一处就会发生——答案是各后继答案的并集。只有当往前的每一条路径都不理它时，寄存器才算已死；这也是为什么在某一条路径上已死的寄存器，在两条路径都会到达的位置上并不已死。

What Olympus needs is one step stricter than "dead at a point". A probe is spliced in inside an island: a short run of instructions written into the kernel's code before instruction i, holding the probe's instructions and, where a probe asks for fewer words than the island reserves, one filler word that is no part of any probe. Chapter 00 introduced the island, and chapter 06, the splicing chapter, is where one is written into the binary. The island runs before instruction i on every path that reaches i, the branches that jump to i included, because those branches are retargeted onto the island's first word (DECISIONS 10) leaders.py:9–11. So "dead before i" has to hold for all of those paths at once api.py:1–25.

Olympus 需要的比"在某个位置已死"还要严一步。探针是装在一个 island 里拼进去的：island 是写进 kernel 代码、位于指令 i 之前的一小段指令，里面装着探针的指令；探针要的字数少于 island 预留的字数时，里面还有一个不属于任何探针的填充字。第 00 章介绍过 island，第 06 章（拼接那一章）把它放进二进制。island 在每条到达 i 的路径上都在指令 i 之前执行，包括跳到 i 的那些分支，因为这些分支被重定向到 island 的第一个字上（DECISIONS 10）leaders.py:9–11。所以"在 i 之前已死"必须对所有这些路径同时成立 api.py:1–25。

The graph the question is asked on在哪张图上问这个问题

Those paths come from the control-flow graph, which the lower half of this chapter's layer builds. A basic block is a straight run of instructions with one way in and one way out: control enters at its first instruction, called the block's leader, and leaves only at its last. The leaders of a kernel are its entry instruction, every target of a branch, call or return, every BSSY target, and the instruction after every control transfer leaders.py:1–7. The control-flow graph, or CFG, is those blocks plus one edge for every way control can get from one block to another.

这些路径来自控制流图，由本章这一层的下半部分构建。基本块是一段只有一个入口、一个出口的顺序指令：控制流从它的第一条指令进入——这条指令叫该块的首指令（leader）——并且只从最后一条离开。一个 kernel 的首指令集合是：入口指令、每个分支 / 调用 / 返回的目标、每个 BSSY 的目标，以及每次控制转移之后的那条指令 leaders.py:1–7。控制流图（CFG）就是这些块，加上控制流可以从一个块到另一个块的每一种方式各一条边。

Three kinds of edge need more than the instruction's own text. A branch through a register (BRX, JMX) carries no literal target, so the graph takes its targets from the cubin's EIATTR_INDIRECT_BRANCH_TARGETS table, which ptxas emits for exactly this purpose (chapter 01). A call gets an edge to the callee's entry and an edge from each return back to every call site; that is context-insensitive, and therefore conservative in the safe direction, since it can only make more registers look live than really are. A register jump with no table entry is the case the graph cannot represent soundly, and there Olympus refuses the kernel instead of guessing graph.py:1–28. Dominators come next, and from them the back edges and natural loops that the next analysis needs, because an operation issued inside a loop body can still be in flight when that body starts again dominators.py:1–10.

有三类边需要的信息不在指令自己的文本里。通过寄存器跳转的分支（BRX、JMX）不带字面目标，所以图从 cubin 的 EIATTR_INDIRECT_BRANCH_TARGETS 表取它的目标，ptxas 正是为此发出这张表（第 01 章）。调用会得到一条到被调者入口的边，以及从每个返回回到每个调用点的边；这是上下文不敏感的，因而是朝安全方向保守的，因为它只会让更多寄存器看起来活跃，不会更少。没有表项的寄存器跳转，是图无法可靠表示的情形，这时 Olympus 拒绝这个 kernel，而不是去猜 graph.py:1–28。接下来是支配关系，由它得到下一项分析需要的回边和自然循环——因为在循环体内发射的操作，到循环体再次开始时可能仍在途中 dominators.py:1–10。

On that graph, the first of five passes is the classical backward equation, iterated until nothing changes analysis.py:289–325. Two SASS details are folded into it: a guarded write may not happen at all, so it does not kill, and an instruction guarded by @!PT never executes, so it has no register effects even though its control word still counts.

在这张图上，五遍分析中的第一遍就是经典的反向方程，迭代到不再变化为止 analysis.py:289–325。其中折进了两处 SASS 细节：带 guard 的写可能根本不发生，所以它不杀死旧值；而以 @!PT 为 guard 的指令永不执行，所以它没有寄存器效应，尽管它的控制字仍然算数。

# the first pass: backward liveness over the CFG, to a fixed point
live_in(i)  = use(i) | (live_out(i) & ~kill(i))   # the answer at the point *before* instruction i
live_out(i) = live_in(s1) | live_in(s2) | ...        # union over i's successors: dead means dead on every path
use(i)      = the registers i reads                 # a read makes a register live before i
kill(i)     = the registers i writes unguarded      # @P0 STG does not kill: the write may not happen

The four register files, and the scoreboards四种寄存器文件，以及 scoreboard

The four register files a probe can use are reported separately and live in one bitset, a single Python integer whose bit positions are fixed per file effects.py:1–16. GPRs, R0 to R254, are the per-lane 32-bit registers that hold almost everything a kernel computes. Uniform registers, UR0 to UR62 on sm_90 and UR0 to UR79 on sm_103, hold one value for the whole warp, and ptxas uses them for addresses, memory descriptors and loop-invariant scalars. Predicates P0 to P6 are the per-lane one-bit registers that guard instructions. Uniform predicates UP0 to UP6 are their one-per-warp counterparts. The convergence barriers a BSSY allocates, the B registers, are not in the bitset at all: they are never offered, so they are never dead.

探针能用的四种寄存器文件分别报告，但共用一个位集——一个 Python 整数，每种文件在其中占固定的比特区间 effects.py:1–16。GPR，R0 到 R254，是按 lane 的 32 位寄存器，kernel 算出来的几乎一切都放在这里。uniform 寄存器，sm_90 上是 UR0 到 UR62、sm_103 上是 UR0 到 UR79，对整个 warp 只有一个值，ptxas 用它们存地址、内存描述符和循环不变的标量。谓词 P0 到 P6 是按 lane 的一位寄存器，用来给指令加 guard。uniform 谓词 UP0 到 UP6 是它们每 warp 一个的对应物。BSSY 分配的汇合屏障，也就是 B 寄存器，根本不在这个位集里：它们从不被交出去，所以也从不是死的。

The scoreboards are tracked beside the registers, and for a related reason. A scoreboard is one of six per-warp counters, SB0 to SB5 (chapter 01), that the hardware uses to track work still in flight; the kernel claims one when it starts a load and waits on it when it needs the result. A probe needs one of its own for the same purpose, and it may not borrow one the kernel is holding, because waiting on that one would make the probe wait for the kernel's load — which is the delay the trace exists to measure (invariant 3). So this layer reports free_scoreboards(i) beside the dead registers: the scoreboards with no outstanding claim at that point. A seventh slot records the operations that claimed no scoreboard at all, which matters because those are the ones whose holds end at an ordinary instruction instead of at a wait.

scoreboard 与寄存器并排跟踪，原因也相关。scoreboard 是每个 warp 六个计数器之一，SB0 到 SB5（第 01 章），硬件用它们跟踪仍在途的工作；kernel 启动一次 load 时占用一个，需要结果时等待它。探针为同样的目的需要一个自己的，而且不能借用 kernel 正占着的那个，因为等待它会让探针去等 kernel 的 load——而那正是追踪本身要测量的延迟（不变量 3）。所以这一层在死寄存器旁边报告 free_scoreboards(i)：该位置上没有未完成占用的 scoreboard。还有第七个槽位记录那些根本没有占用 scoreboard 的操作，这很重要，因为它们的占用是在一条普通指令处结束，而不是在一次等待处结束。

Registers that look dead and are not看似已死、其实不然的寄存器

Everything so far would be enough on a machine where an instruction is finished by the time the next one issues. Hopper is not that machine, and the gap is where this layer earns its name. Four more passes run over the same graph, and each adds registers back to the live set that the backward pass had already written off. Their results are the holds: the registers an instruction that has already issued still owns at the point we are asking about, joined over paths and followed across back edges analysis.py:327–365. Every hold is reported with its issuing instruction, its kind and its release point, which is what the heatmap in §5 reads for its hover text holds.py:1–24.

到此为止的一切，在"下一条指令发射时上一条就已完成"的机器上就够了。Hopper 不是那种机器，而这个差距正是这一层得名之处。还有四遍分析跑在同一张图上，每一遍都把反向分析已经勾销的寄存器重新加回活跃集合。它们的结果就是占用（holds）：当控制流到达我们询问的位置时，已经发射的指令仍然占有的寄存器，沿路径取并集，并跨过回边继续跟踪 analysis.py:327–365。每个占用都连同它的发射指令、种类和释放点一起报告，§5 的热力图读的就是这些做悬停文字 holds.py:1–24。

The first kind of hold is the destination of an operation whose latency is not fixed. LDG, the global load, is the plain example: it issues, and the hardware writes its destination register hundreds of cycles later, when the data arrives from memory. In between, nothing in the instruction stream reads that register, so the backward pass calls it dead — and the hardware is about to write it. The load therefore claims a write scoreboard, and the destination is held from the load's issue until some instruction waits on that scoreboard. If the load claimed none, the hold runs until the kernel itself next reads or writes the register unguarded, because ptxas must have known the value had landed by then effects.py:23–30.

第一类占用是延迟不固定的操作的目的寄存器。全局 load LDG 是最朴素的例子：它发射出去，而硬件要在数百周期之后、数据从内存回来时才写它的目的寄存器。在这期间，指令流里没有任何东西读这个寄存器，于是反向分析判它已死——而硬件正要写它。所以 load 占用一个写 scoreboard，目的寄存器从 load 发射起被占用，直到某条指令等待这个 scoreboard。如果这次 load 没有占用任何 scoreboard，占用就持续到 kernel 自己下一次无 guard 地读或写这个寄存器，因为 ptxas 必定知道那时值已经落地 effects.py:23–30。

The second kind is the mirror image: the sources of an operation in flight. A load reads its address registers after it issues as well, not at the moment of issue. Overwriting one of them before the hardware has read it changes the address the load fetches from, which gives a wrong value or an illegal access rather than a stale one. So a source of a variable-latency, memory or asynchronous instruction is held until that instruction's read scoreboard is waited on, or, when it set no read scoreboard, until its write scoreboard is effects.py:23–30. This is the case the worked example below walks through, and it is the easiest one to miss by eye. The kernel has no reason to protect those registers, since ptxas knows the kernel will not overwrite them, so nothing in the kernel's text marks the end of the window.

第二类是它的镜像：在途操作的源操作数。load 读自己的地址寄存器也是在发射之后，而不是在发射那一刻。在硬件读到它之前改写其中之一，会改变 load 取数的地址，得到的是错的值或非法访问，而不是过期的值。所以变延迟、内存或异步指令的源操作数被占用，直到这条指令的读 scoreboard 被等待；如果它没有设读 scoreboard，就到它的写 scoreboard 被等待为止 effects.py:23–30。下面的实例走的就是这一种，而它也是肉眼最容易漏掉的一种。kernel 没有理由保护这些寄存器，因为 ptxas 知道 kernel 不会改写它们，所以 kernel 的文本里没有任何东西标出这个窗口的结束。

When an operand has no scoreboard at all, the hold ends at the kernel's own next access to the register, and there one subtlety earned its own ruling. The releasing access counts not only when it is unguarded but also when it carries the issuer's own guard: the same predicate with the same polarity, with nothing on the path between having written that predicate. A lane that ran the issuer had that predicate true, so with the predicate unchanged it runs the releasing access too (DECISIONS 89) effects.py:31–39. Without that rule, DeepGEMM's fp8 kernel kept an @!P0 LDS R8 pending through the @!P0 STS.128 that consumes it, and R8 stayed excluded for the rest of the kernel for no reason.

当某个操作数完全没有 scoreboard 时，占用在 kernel 自己下一次访问该寄存器处结束，而这里有一处细节值得单独定规则。释放性的访问不仅在它无 guard 时算，在它带着发射者自己的 guard 时也算：同一个谓词、同样的极性，并且两者之间的路径上没有任何指令写过这个谓词。执行过发射者的 lane 当时这个谓词为真，所以在谓词未变的情况下，它也会执行这次释放性访问（DECISIONS 89）effects.py:31–39。没有这条规则时，DeepGEMM 的 fp8 kernel 里一条 @!P0 LDS R8 会一直挂着，穿过消费它的 @!P0 STS.128，R8 在该 kernel 余下的部分被无谓地排除在外。

Tensor-core work holds registers for far longer than a load does. A wgmma is an asynchronous warpgroup matrix multiply: one instruction, HGMMA / QGMMA / IGMMA / BGMMA in SASS, starts a tile of multiply-accumulate across the four warps of a warpgroup, and the warp runs on while it executes. Its accumulators are a block of GPRs it reads and writes — 32 of them in t1_attention's HGMMA.64x64x16.F32, the registers R56 to R87 — and its A operand may be a register fragment rather than shared memory. Every one of those GPRs is held from issue until a WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, N retires its group: the instructions since the last commit form the open group, a GMMA carrying gsb0 commits it, and the DEPBAR retires every committed group but the N newest. WARPGROUP.ARRIVE and WARPGROUP.WAIT release nothing effects.py:40–44. The hold routinely crosses a back edge, which means the release can be in the next iteration of the loop (DECISIONS 25).

tensor core 的工作占用寄存器的时间比一次 load 长得多。wgmma 是异步的 warpgroup 矩阵乘：一条指令（SASS 里是 HGMMA / QGMMA / IGMMA / BGMMA）在一个 warpgroup 的四个 warp 上启动一块乘加，而 warp 在它执行期间继续往下跑。它的累加器是它要读写的一批 GPR——在 t1_attention 的 HGMMA.64x64x16.F32 里是 32 个，寄存器 R56 到 R87——而它的 A 操作数可能是寄存器片段而不是共享内存。这些 GPR 每一个都从发射起被占用，直到一条 WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, N 让它所在的组退休：自上次提交以来的指令构成当前打开的组，带 gsb0 的 GMMA 提交该组，而 DEPBAR 让除最新 N 个之外的所有已提交组退休。WARPGROUP.ARRIVE 和 WARPGROUP.WAIT 什么都不释放 effects.py:40–44。这种占用经常跨过回边，也就是说释放点可能在循环的下一轮迭代里（DECISIONS 25）。

A wgmma also holds its registers before it issues, which is the one place Olympus is stricter than the paper. WARPGROUP.ARRIVE is the SASS form of wgmma.fence, and PTX requires such a fence between any register access by the thread and a later wgmma that touches the same registers; without it, "the behavior is undefined" (ISA 9.7.17.7.1). So the fourth pass runs backward from each GMMA to the fence that covers it and marks every register that GMMA accesses as not dead in between, including accumulators the GMMA only writes analysis.py:453–492 effects.py:45–49. In the attention window of §5 this shows up as a cliff. The point before instruction 293, the WARPGROUP.ARRIVE itself, has 51 dead GPRs; the point before 294 has 10, because the six HGMMAs that follow access R24 to R87 somewhere between the fence and themselves. The count returns to 42 at instruction 327, one past the WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x0 that retires the group.

wgmma 在发射之前也占用它的寄存器，这是 Olympus 比论文更严的唯一一处。WARPGROUP.ARRIVE 是 wgmma.fence 的 SASS 形式，而 PTX 要求：线程对某些寄存器的任何访问，与之后访问同一批寄存器的 wgmma 之间，必须有这样一道 fence；否则"行为未定义"（ISA 9.7.17.7.1）。所以第四遍从每个 GMMA 反向走到覆盖它的那道 fence，把该 GMMA 访问的每个寄存器在这段区间内标为不死，包括 GMMA 只写不读的累加器 analysis.py:453–492 effects.py:45–49。在 §5 的 attention 窗口里，这表现为一道断崖。指令 293（也就是那条 WARPGROUP.ARRIVE）之前的位置有 51 个死 GPR；294 之前的位置只有 10 个，因为紧随其后的六条 HGMMA 在 fence 与它们自己之间的某处访问 R24 到 R87。到指令 327，也就是让该组退休的 WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x0 之后一条，计数回到 42。

Blackwell adds a second asynchronous engine, and a memory to go with it. The tcgen05 instructions compute in tensor memory, a per-SM scratch space separate from the register file, so the registers they touch are descriptors, addresses and load destinations rather than accumulators. The same late-operand rule covers them: an LDTM destination is held until tcgen05.wait::ld, and an asynchronous MMA's descriptor registers until its read scoreboard is released. Fourteen SASS classes have established register effects and are modelled by name; a kernel containing any other tcgen05 or tensor-memory class is refused with UnsupportedKernel, so no register of it is ever reported dead effects.py:86–95. Refusing is the safe direction: a kernel we cannot model yields no probes, rather than wrong ones (DECISIONS 85–89).

Blackwell 增加了第二个异步引擎，还有与之配套的一块内存。tcgen05 指令在 tensor memory 里计算——这是每个 SM 独有、与寄存器文件分开的暂存空间——所以它们碰到的寄存器是描述符、地址和 load 的目的寄存器，而不是累加器。同一条延迟操作数规则覆盖它们：LDTM 的目的寄存器被占用到 tcgen05.wait::ld，异步 MMA 的描述符寄存器到它的读 scoreboard 被释放为止。有 14 个 SASS 类的寄存器效应已经确立，按名字建模；含有任何其他 tcgen05 或 tensor memory 类的 kernel 以 UnsupportedKernel 拒绝，于是它的任何寄存器都不会被报告为已死 effects.py:86–95。拒绝是安全的方向：模型覆盖不到的 kernel 得到的是没有探针，而不是错的探针（DECISIONS 85–89）。

Two further terms are about the allocation itself rather than about values. USETMAXREG is the instruction a warp uses to change its own register budget, which is how a warp-specialized kernel gives its producer warps a small budget and its consumer warps a large one and lets them trade at runtime. USETMAXREG.DEALLOC n lowers the warp's cap to n and returns the difference to the pool; USETMAXREG.TRY_ALLOC n asks for a raise to n, which takes effect only on the success edge of its retry loop (DECISIONS 33). A register above the cap in force is not the warp's to write, so the cap is a forward must analysis: the minimum over all paths that reach the point analysis.py:367–408. DeepGEMM's k512 kernel shows the step plainly. The cap in force is 168 at instruction 1112, which is USETMAXREG.DEALLOC.CTAPOOL 0x28, and 40 from instruction 1113 onward; the dead-GPR count falls from 164 to 36 across that one boundary, and 164 − 36 is exactly the 128 registers the warp has just handed back.

还有两项关乎分配本身，而不是关乎值。USETMAXREG 是 warp 用来改变自己寄存器预算的指令；warp 专门化的 kernel 正是靠它给 producer warp 小预算、给 consumer warp 大预算，并让它们在运行时互相交换。USETMAXREG.DEALLOC n 把该 warp 的上限降到 n，把差额还给池子；USETMAXREG.TRY_ALLOC n 申请抬高到 n，而它只在其重试循环的成功边上生效（DECISIONS 33）。高于当前生效上限的寄存器不属于这个 warp，不能写，所以上限是一个正向的 must 分析：取所有到达该位置的路径上的最小值 analysis.py:367–408。DeepGEMM 的 k512 kernel 把这个台阶摆得很清楚。在指令 1112（也就是 USETMAXREG.DEALLOC.CTAPOOL 0x28）处生效的上限是 168，从指令 1113 起是 40；死 GPR 计数在这一个边界上从 164 掉到 36，而 164 − 36 正好是这个 warp 刚刚交还的 128 个寄存器。

On top of the cap, both architectures reserve the top two GPRs of whatever allocation is in force. No corpus kernel names them, and an island that writes either one faults with an illegal instruction — measured at REGCOUNT 16, at REGCOUNT 255, and at DeepGEMM's caps of 40 and 232 (DECISIONS 44) analysis.py:66–72. REGCOUNT is the per-kernel register allocation the compiler recorded in the cubin (chapter 01), so in t1_axpy, whose REGCOUNT is 26, the two reserved GPRs are R24 and R25 and a probe can only ever use R0 to R23.

在上限之上，两种架构都保留当前生效分配顶端的两个 GPR。没有一个语料库 kernel 用到它们，而写其中任何一个的 island 都会以非法指令出错——在 REGCOUNT 16、REGCOUNT 255，以及 DeepGEMM 的上限 40 和 232 上都测过（DECISIONS 44）analysis.py:66–72。REGCOUNT 是编译器记在 cubin 里的每个 kernel 的寄存器分配数（第 01 章），所以在 REGCOUNT 为 26 的 t1_axpy 里，两个保留 GPR 是 R24 和 R25，探针永远只能用 R0 到 R23。

The last term is about uniform registers, and about the fact that a warp is not always in one place. Independent thread scheduling lets a warp split into lane groups that run different code at the same time, and a UR or UP is one register for the whole warp. An island that one group executes at point p overwrites the uniform registers of every other group as well. So a UR is dead before p only if it is dead at p and at every point another group may be at while this group is at p. The fifth pass computes those concurrent points. A divergence region starts at a branch that can split a group and ends at a cut: an instruction no lane passes until every non-exited lane of the warp has arrived there, such as WARPSYNC.ALL or the BSYNC of a convergence region. BAR.SYNC is not a cut, because it synchronizes the whole block rather than the warp's lanes divergence.py:1–27. Branches that provably cannot split a group are excluded by rule: a .U branch reduces its guard over the group and so jumps or falls through as a whole, a .DIV branch with no per-lane operand decides per group, and a branch whose per-lane operands are all equal across the group cannot separate two of its lanes divergence.py:29–41.

最后一项关乎 uniform 寄存器，也关乎一个事实：warp 并不总是在同一个位置上。独立线程调度允许一个 warp 分裂成若干 lane 组，同时执行不同的代码，而一个 UR 或 UP 对整个 warp 只有一个。一组 lane 在位置 p 执行的 island，同时也改写了其他每一组的 uniform 寄存器。所以一个 UR 在 p 之前是死的，当且仅当它在 p 处是死的，并且在本组位于 p 时另一组可能所在的每个位置上也是死的。第五遍就是算这些并发位置。一个分叉区域从可能分裂一组 lane 的分支开始，到一个切口（cut）结束：切口是这样一条指令，在 warp 中每个未退出的 lane 都到达它之前，没有任何 lane 能越过它，例如 WARPSYNC.ALL，或者某个汇合区域的 BSYNC。BAR.SYNC 不是切口，因为它同步的是整个 block，不是 warp 内的 lane divergence.py:1–27。可以证明不会分裂一组 lane 的分支按规则排除在外：带 .U 的分支把 guard 在整组上归约，于是整组一起跳转或一起继续；带 .DIV 且没有按 lane 操作数的分支是按组做决定；而按 lane 操作数在组内处处相等的分支，不可能把组里的两个 lane 分开 divergence.py:29–41。

The answer is one mask per point. A register is dead before i when it is inside the allocation in force at i, is not one of the two reserved GPRs, and is in none of the four live sets. why_live names which term excluded a register, which is what the heatmap shows on hover analysis.py:498–510 analysis.py:529–551:

答案是每个位置一个掩码。寄存器在 i 之前是死的，当且仅当它位于 i 处生效的分配范围之内、不是那两个保留 GPR，并且不属于那四个活跃集合中的任何一个；why_live 说出是哪一项排除了某个寄存器，热力图悬停时显示的就是它 analysis.py:498–510 analysis.py:529–551：

# olympus/liveness/analysis.py, the Liveness protocol
def live_mask(self, i):          # registers not dead before i, ignoring the allocation cap
    return self.live_in[i] | self.held[i] | self.fence_in[i] | self.div_extra[i]

def dead_mask(self, i):          # registers dead before i: GPRs below the cap in force at i
    alloc = (_cap_mask(self.cap_in[i]) & GPR_MASK) | _ureg_mask(max_ureg) | PRED | UPRED
    return alloc & ~self.live_mask(i)

One register, one point一个寄存器，一个位置

The rules are easier to trust against one real case. Here is the whole of it on t1_axpy, the simplest kernel in the corpus: 42 instructions, one basic block, every instruction reachable, REGCOUNT 26, so R0 to R23 usable and R24, R25 reserved. These are the seven instructions that matter, with their real byte offsets (16 bytes per instruction, chapter 01) and their real control words, printed as [wr:rd:{wait}:stall:yield] with 7 meaning no scoreboard:

把规则放到一个真实案例上更容易信得过。下面是它在 t1_axpy 上的全部：语料库里最简单的 kernel，42 条指令，一个基本块，每条指令都可达，REGCOUNT 26，于是 R0 到 R23 可用、R24 和 R25 保留。真正要紧的是这七条指令，连同它们真实的字节偏移（每条指令 16 字节，第 01 章）和真实的控制字，控制字按 [wr:rd:{wait}:stall:yield] 打印，7 表示不用 scoreboard：

  1. Instruction 19 builds an address. IMAD.WIDE multiplies the thread's index in R0 by 4 and adds it to a base pointer, leaving a 64-bit address in the register pair {R20,R21}. From here on R20 holds the low half of that address, and it is the last unguarded write to R20 in the kernel.
  2. Instructions 25 and 27 read it. Both are 128-bit global loads that use {R20,R21} as their address, the second with a +0x800 displacement. Each claims a write scoreboard for its destination — SB2 at 25, SB3 at 27 — and neither claims a read scoreboard, so by the rule above their address registers are held until the write scoreboard is waited on.
  3. Instruction 27 is the last read of R20 in program order. Walk backward from the EXIT at instruction 41: nothing after 27 names R20 or R21 again. So the backward pass has R20 live at the point before 27 and not live at the point before 28, byte offset 0x1c0. On the textbook definition R20 is now free.
  4. It is not free. The load at 27 has issued, and nothing yet guarantees the hardware has read its address. The hold analysis records {R20,R21} held from 27, kind rd-sb-src, scoreboard SB3; the load at 25 leaves a second hold on the same pair with SB2. Both are open at the point before 28.
  5. The release point is instruction 34. FFMA R4, R4, UR6, R8 carries wait mask {3}, which is the kernel waiting for the load at 27 to deliver R4. That wait is not there for R20 at all; it frees R20 as a side effect. So the pair is still held at the point before 34, where the wait has not yet executed, and dead at the point before 35. The window is seven points wide: 28 through 34.
  6. The verdict at the point before 28: no GPR is dead. dead_before(28) reports 0 of the 24 usable GPRs, 60 of the 63 uniform registers (UR4 and UR5 carry the memory descriptor, UR6 the scalar a), 5 of the 7 predicates (P0 and P1 guard the loads) and all 7 uniform predicates. Five consecutive points, 25 through 29, have no dead GPR at all, which is why §5 describes this window as the one where only uniform registers are free.
  7. What the holds were worth here. Drop them and exactly three GPRs become dead at this point: R20, R21, and R1 — held from instruction 0's LDC R1, c[0x0][0x28], a constant load with no scoreboard whose value this kernel never reads, so its hold has no release and runs to the exit. A probe handed R20 and R21 would have written over an address the hardware had not finished reading, and the load at 27 would have fetched from somewhere else entirely or faulted. Neither failure names R20 anywhere in the kernel's output, which is the whole argument for checking the contract by running the kernel.

  2. 指令 25 和 27 读它。两条都是 128 位全局 load，用 {R20,R21} 作地址，第二条带 +0x800 的位移。每条都为自己的目的寄存器占用一个写 scoreboard——25 占 SB2，27 占 SB3——而两条都没有占读 scoreboard，所以按上面那条规则，它们的地址寄存器被占用到写 scoreboard 被等待为止。
  3. 按程序顺序，指令 27 是对 R20 的最后一次读。从指令 41 的 EXIT 往回走：27 之后没有任何指令再提到 R20 或 R21。所以反向分析在 27 之前的位置上认为 R20 活跃，在 28 之前的位置（字节偏移 0x1c0）认为它不活跃。按教科书的定义，R20 现在空闲了。
  4. 它并不空闲。27 处的 load 已经发射，而此时还没有任何东西保证硬件已经读过它的地址。占用分析记下：{R20,R21} 自 27 起被占用，种类 rd-sb-src，scoreboard 为 SB3；25 处的 load 在同一对寄存器上留下第二个占用，用 SB2。在 28 之前的位置上，两个占用都还开着。
  5. 释放点是指令 34。FFMA R4, R4, UR6, R8 带着等待掩码 {3}，那是 kernel 在等 27 处的 load 把 R4 送到。这次等待根本不是为 R20 设的；它只是顺带释放了 R20。所以在 34 之前的位置上这对寄存器仍被占用——那里等待还没执行——而在 35 之前的位置上已死。窗口宽七个位置：28 到 34。
  6. 28 之前这个位置上的判定：没有一个 GPR 是死的。dead_before(28) 报告：24 个可用 GPR 中 0 个，63 个 uniform 寄存器中 60 个（UR4 和 UR5 装着内存描述符，UR6 装着标量 a），7 个谓词中 5 个（P0 和 P1 给两条 load 当 guard），以及全部 7 个 uniform 谓词。连续五个位置、25 到 29，一个死 GPR 也没有；这正是 §5 把这个窗口描述为"只有 uniform 寄存器空闲"的原因。
  7. 这些占用在这里值多少。去掉它们，这个位置上正好有三个 GPR 变成死的：R20、R21，以及 R1——R1 被指令 0 的 LDC R1, c[0x0][0x28] 占着，那是一次没有 scoreboard 的常量 load，而这个 kernel 从不读它的值，所以这个占用没有释放点，一直延到出口。拿到 R20 和 R21 的探针，会覆盖掉一个硬件还没读完的地址，于是 27 处的 load 会从完全别的地方取数，或者直接出错。这两种失败都不会在 kernel 的输出里提到 R20 一个字，而这正是"用运行 kernel 的方式检查这条契约"的全部理由。

How the contract is checked契约如何被检查

The dead-register check does not inspect the analysis; it tries to break the kernel with it. At every reachable instruction of a corpus kernel it splices an island that sets every register dead_before(i) reports, in all four files, to a test value. Then it runs the kernel on its usual inputs and compares every output byte with the stored reference. Two phases write complementary values, 0x5a5a5a5a with the predicates set true and 0xa5a5a5a5 with them false, so every bit of every overwritten register takes both values. The islands use immediates only and claim no scoreboard, so they add no hazard of their own. A batch whose outputs differ, or that faults or hangs, is bisected to a single point and then to a minimal set of registers, which is what turns a failure into a rule deadcheck.py:1–40.

死寄存器检查不审视分析；它用分析的结论去尝试破坏 kernel。在语料库 kernel 的每条可达指令处，它拼入一个 island，把 dead_before(i) 在四种文件里报告的每个寄存器都设为一个测试值。然后用平常的输入运行 kernel，把每个输出字节与保存的参考比较。两个阶段写入互补的值：0x5a5a5a5a 配谓词为真，0xa5a5a5a5 配谓词为假，于是每个被改写寄存器的每一位都取过两种值。island 只用立即数、不占用任何 scoreboard，因此不会带进自己的冒险。输出不一致、出错或挂起的批次会被二分到单个位置，再二分到最小的寄存器集合，正是这一步把一次失败变成一条规则 deadcheck.py:1–40。

The scale matters, because the claim is about every point and not about a sample. Every reachable instruction is a test point: 42 of them in t1_axpy, 7,457 in t3_flashmla_decode athena.md:124–135. On the GB300 T0 kernels the islands overwrote 75,248 GPRs, 132,124 uniform registers, 11,702 predicates and 11,670 uniform predicates summed over 1,710 points, with mean dead uniform registers per point running from 76.2 to 78.4 of 80 athena.md:595–600. The island words themselves are checked three ways — Olympus's own decoder, the offline splice proof, and cuobjdump's text of the spliced image — and matched on 4,977,610 instructions through the runtime and 450,932 offline, with 0 differences athena.md:422.

规模很重要，因为这个断言是关于每一个位置，而不是关于一个样本。每条可达指令都是一个测试点：t1_axpy 有 42 个，t3_flashmla_decode 有 7,457 个 athena.md:124–135。在 GB300 的 T0 kernel 上，各 island 在 1,710 个位置上合计改写了 75,248 个 GPR、132,124 个 uniform 寄存器、11,702 个谓词和 11,670 个 uniform 谓词，每个位置平均死掉的 uniform 寄存器在 80 个中占 76.2 到 78.4 个 athena.md:595–600。island 本身的指令字用三种方式核对——Olympus 自己的解码器、离线拼接证明，以及 cuobjdump 对拼接后镜像的文本——经运行时核对了 4,977,610 条指令、离线 450,932 条，0 处不同 athena.md:422。

A check that cannot fail proves nothing, so every case also runs controls: islands that deliberately overwrite a register the analysis calls live, namely a store's data GPRs, the predicate that guards the store, and the uniform register holding its address. These must change the outputs, and they do. On the GB300 T0 kernels the GPR control changes the outputs in 10 of 10 cases, the uniform-register control in all 9 cases that have a candidate (a fault in 6, a different result in 3), and the predicate control — P2 guarding a store in t0_predstore — changes them too athena.md:595–600. The controls are what make a pass mean something: they show that the islands really executed on that launch path and really wrote their registers.

不可能失败的检查什么也证明不了，所以每个用例还要跑对照：故意改写分析判为活跃的寄存器的 island，具体是 store 的数据 GPR、给这条 store 当 guard 的谓词，以及装着它地址的 uniform 寄存器。这些必须改变输出，而它们确实改变了。在 GB300 的 T0 kernel 上，GPR 对照在 10 个用例中 10 个改变了输出，uniform 寄存器对照在有候选的全部 9 个用例中都改变了（6 个出错、3 个结果不同），谓词对照——t0_predstore 里给一条 store 当 guard 的 P2——也改变了输出 athena.md:595–600。对照正是让"通过"有意义的东西：它们表明这些 island 真的在那条启动路径上执行了，也真的写了自己的寄存器。

A pass is evidence, not proof. The fence bug showed in 8 runs of 9, so a timing-dependent hazard could hide in a single run of a case. That is why the two rules this check found on its way, the fence window and the two reserved GPRs, each rest on a PTX contract or a measurement of their own, rather than on the check that happened to find them.

通过只是证据，不是证明。fence 的问题在 9 次运行里出现了 8 次，所以一个与时序有关的冒险完全可能藏在某个用例的单次运行里。这正是为什么这项检查一路发现的两条规则——fence 窗口和两个保留的 GPR——各自以 PTX 的约定或一次专门的测量为依据，而不以恰好发现它们的那次检查为依据。

§4Where it came from从何而来

The paper names this the first challenge of splicing: the probe may overwrite a register that still holds a live value, and the binary does not say which registers are safe paper §2.3. Its answer is the one Olympus keeps. A backward liveness analysis over the kernel's control flow gives each register its live periods, a probe borrows registers in the gaps, and the registers of asynchronous operations such as wgmma are marked live until the operation completes paper §4.3. The paper then asks whether dead-period registers are enough to feed the probes. On its three NVIDIA kernels they were: every splice was fed without a fallback and without adding a register, with 6 to 9 registers still free at the tightest insertion points paper §5.5. Olympus's corpus has tighter points than that — t1_axpy has five in a row with no dead GPR at all — which is why its planner has a per-warp fallback, raises REGCOUNT only when asked to, and otherwise refuses the site.

论文把这列为拼接的第一个挑战：探针可能改写一个仍装着活跃值的寄存器，而二进制没有说明哪些寄存器是安全的 论文 §2.3。它的答案就是 Olympus 保留的那个。对 kernel 的控制流做反向活跃性分析，得到每个寄存器的活跃区间，探针在区间之间的空档里借用寄存器，而 wgmma 这类异步操作的寄存器被标为活跃，直到操作完成 论文 §4.3。论文随后考察死区间的寄存器是否够喂给探针。在它的三个 NVIDIA kernel 上是够的：每一次拼接都只靠它们完成，既没有回退，也没有增加寄存器，最紧的插入点上仍有 6 到 9 个寄存器空闲 论文 §5.5。Olympus 的语料库里有比这更紧的位置——t1_axpy 就有连续五个位置一个死 GPR 都没有——所以它的规划器有按 warp 的回退，只在被要求时才抬高 REGCOUNT，否则就拒绝该站点。
